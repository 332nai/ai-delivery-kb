# Long-form and Articles

30 prompts for structuring, drafting, and polishing essays, blog posts, and reports.

## Structure and outline

1. Outline a 1500-word article on [topic] with a clear narrative arc.
2. Give me 3 different angles to write an article about [topic].
3. Propose a contrarian thesis on [topic] and outline supporting points.
4. Build a 5-section outline for a thought leadership piece on [subject].
5. Suggest a hook, middle, and ending for an essay on [topic].
6. Structure a how-to guide on [task] with prerequisites and steps.
7. Outline a case study about [project], use the SCQA framework.
8. Build a comparison article structure: [X] vs [Y] vs [Z].
9. Outline a deep-dive article on [topic] for a technical audience.
10. Suggest 5 sub-questions to answer in an article about [main question].

## Drafting

11. Write an opening hook (3 sentences) for an article on [topic].
12. Draft section [N] of an article: [section title], ~250 words.
13. Write the conclusion paragraph for an article that argued [thesis].
14. Generate 5 anecdote starters relevant to an article on [topic].
15. Draft 3 statistical claims I could use to support [argument], with sources.
16. Write a transition paragraph between [section A] and [section B].
17. Draft a side note / callout box about [tangential point] for an article.
18. Write a Q&A-style segment within an article about [topic].
19. Draft a section that addresses common objections to [thesis].
20. Write a personal-story opener for an article on [professional topic].

## Polishing

21. Identify weak transitions in my draft and suggest replacements: [paste].
22. Tighten my draft — find sentences that can be cut by 30%: [paste].
23. Find logical gaps in my argument: [paste].
24. Suggest a more compelling title for [article topic and current title].
25. Find passive voice and convert to active: [paste].
26. Suggest where to add subheadings to break up [paste].
27. Check this for repetitive words and phrases: [paste].
28. Suggest 3 stronger opening lines for [article]: [paste current opener].
29. Make my conclusion stickier — what's the one line readers should remember?
30. Suggest one image, chart, or pull-quote for each section of [draft].

**Total prompts: 30**

# 长文与文章

30 条 prompt: 文章、博客、报告的结构、起草、润色。

## 结构与大纲

1. 为 [话题] 起草一篇 1500 字文章的大纲,要有清晰的叙事弧线。
2. 给我 [话题] 三个不同的切入角度。
3. 为 [话题] 提一个反向论点,列出支持要点。
4. 为 [主题] 思想领导力文章构建一个 5 节大纲。
5. 为 [话题] 文章建议开头、中段、结尾。
6. 为 [任务] 起草一篇 how-to 指南,含前提和步骤。
7. 用 SCQA 框架为 [项目] 起草案例研究大纲。
8. 构建一篇对比文章结构: [X] 对 [Y] 对 [Z]。
9. 为技术受众起草一篇 [话题] 深度文章的大纲。
10. 为关于 [主问题] 的文章建议 5 个要回答的子问题。

## 起草

11. 为 [话题] 文章写一个 3 句话的开头钩子。
12. 起草文章第 [N] 节: [小节标题],约 250 字。
13. 为论证 [主题] 的文章写结尾段。
14. 为 [话题] 文章生成 5 个相关的小故事开头。
15. 为 [论点] 起草 3 个统计性论据,附来源。
16. 在 [A 节] 和 [B 节] 之间写一段过渡。
17. 为文章起草一个关于 [侧线点] 的旁注/小框。
18. 在 [话题] 文章里写一段 Q&A 风格的内容。
19. 起草一节,回应对 [主题] 的常见反对意见。
20. 为 [专业话题] 文章写一个个人故事式开头。

## 润色

21. 找出我草稿里的弱过渡并提建议: [粘贴]。
22. 精简我的草稿 — 找出能砍 30% 的句子: [粘贴]。
23. 找出我论证里的逻辑漏洞: [粘贴]。
24. 为 [文章主题和当前标题] 提一个更吸引人的标题。
25. 找出被动语态并改成主动: [粘贴]。
26. 建议 [粘贴] 在哪些地方加小标题来分段。
27. 检查这段是否有重复的词和短语: [粘贴]。
28. 为 [文章] 提议 3 个更有力的开头句: [粘贴现开头]。
29. 让我的结尾更难忘 — 读者该记住的那一句是什么?
30. 为 [草稿] 每一节建议一张配图、图表或金句。

**共 30 条 prompt**
