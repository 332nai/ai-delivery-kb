# Emails

30 reusable email prompts: drafting, replying, and editing. Replace `[bracketed]` parts with your specifics.

## Drafting from scratch

1. Write an email to [recipient] about [topic], tone [formal/casual], under [N] words.
2. Draft a cold outreach email to [target] introducing [product/service] in 3 short paragraphs.
3. Write a follow-up email to [name] who hasn't replied to [previous email] for [N] days.
4. Draft an introduction email connecting [person A] and [person B], explain why they'd benefit.
5. Write a meeting request email to [person], propose [3 time slots], state purpose in one line.
6. Draft a status update email to [stakeholder] covering: progress, blockers, next steps.
7. Write a thank-you email after [meeting/interview/help], be specific about what helped.
8. Write an apology email for [mistake], own it without over-apologizing, give a fix.
9. Draft an announcement email to [team/list] about [news], keep under 150 words.
10. Write a resignation email to [manager], 2 paragraphs, grateful and concrete on dates.

## Replying

11. Reply politely declining [request], suggest [alternative] if relevant, paste: [email].
12. Reply confirming receipt of [document/request], state next steps and timeline.
13. Draft a reply acknowledging [criticism] without being defensive: [paste].
14. Reply asking [3 clarifying questions] before committing to [request].
15. Reply postponing [meeting/deadline] from [old] to [new], explain briefly, propose options.
16. Draft a reply pushing back on [unrealistic request] while keeping the relationship warm.
17. Reply forwarding [request] to [right person] with a 2-sentence handoff.
18. Write a brief reply saying 'received, will get back by [date]', nothing more.
19. Reply to an angry customer about [issue], empathize first, then offer [fix].
20. Draft a reply gracefully accepting [offer/invitation], confirm 3 details.

## Editing existing emails

21. Shorten this email by 50%, keep all the actionable parts: [paste].
22. Rewrite this email to sound more confident and less apologetic: [paste].
23. Make this email warmer without losing professionalism: [paste].
24. Rewrite this in plain English, remove all jargon: [paste].
25. Add a clear ask at the end of this email — what action by when: [paste].
26. Convert this dense paragraph into 3-bullet format: [paste].
27. Rewrite this email with the bottom line in the first sentence: [paste].
28. Fix tone in this email — current reads as [too aggressive/too soft]: [paste].
29. Translate this email into [target language], keep the tone consistent: [paste].
30. Proofread this email for grammar and clarity, mark changes: [paste].

# 邮件

30 条邮件 prompt: 起草、回复、编辑。把 `[方括号]` 部分换成你的具体内容即可。

## 从零起草

1. 给 [收件人] 写一封关于 [主题] 的邮件,语气 [正式/随意],不超过 [N] 字。
2. 给 [目标对象] 写一封陌生开发邮件,介绍 [产品/服务],三段以内。
3. 给 [姓名] 写一封跟进邮件,对方 [N] 天没回复 [上一封邮件]。
4. 写一封引荐邮件,把 [甲] 介绍给 [乙],说明为什么对双方有益。
5. 给 [对象] 写一封约会邮件,提议 [3 个时间段],一句话说明目的。
6. 给 [干系人] 写一封进展邮件: 涵盖进展、阻碍、下一步。
7. 写一封 [会议/面试/帮助] 后的致谢邮件,具体说明哪一点对你最有帮助。
8. 写一封 [失误] 的道歉邮件,承担责任但不过度道歉,给修复方案。
9. 给 [团队/邮件组] 写一封 [消息] 通知邮件,控制在 150 字以内。
10. 写一封致 [上级] 的辞职邮件,两段,有谢意,日期具体。

## 回复

11. 礼貌拒绝 [请求],可行的话提议 [替代方案]。原邮件: [邮件]。
12. 回复确认收到 [文件/请求],说明下一步和时间线。
13. 起草一封对 [批评] 的回复,不防御。原文: [粘贴]。
14. 在承诺 [请求] 前,回复提出 [3 个澄清问题]。
15. 回复延期 [会议/截止日期] 从 [旧] 到 [新],简要说明,给替代选项。
16. 起草一封回复,对 [不合理请求] 推回,但保持关系融洽。
17. 回复转发 [请求] 给 [对的人],附两句移交说明。
18. 写一封简短回复 — 已收到,[日期] 前给答复,不多说。
19. 回复一封愤怒客户邮件关于 [问题],先共情,再给 [解决方案]。
20. 起草一封优雅接受 [邀请/offer] 的回复,确认 3 个细节。

## 改写现有邮件

21. 把这封邮件缩短一半,保留所有可执行部分: [粘贴]。
22. 改写这封邮件,语气更自信、少道歉: [粘贴]。
23. 让这封邮件更温暖,但不失专业: [粘贴]。
24. 用通俗英语改写,去掉所有术语: [粘贴]。
25. 在邮件末尾加一个明确的请求 — 谁在什么时间做什么: [粘贴]。
26. 把这段密集的文字改成 3 个要点: [粘贴]。
27. 改写这封邮件,把核心结论放到第一句: [粘贴]。
28. 调整这封邮件的语气 — 现在显得 [太冲/太软]: [粘贴]。
29. 把这封邮件翻译成 [目标语言],保持语气一致: [粘贴]。
30. 校对这封邮件的语法和清晰度,标出修改: [粘贴]。
