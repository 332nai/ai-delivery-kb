**Definition**

Chunking is the process of splitting documents into smaller pieces before embedding. A chunk is the unit that gets retrieved. Strategies range from naive fixed size splits (every 500 tokens), to structural splits (by paragraph, section, or heading), to semantic chunking (splits at meaning boundaries), to hierarchical (small chunks for retrieval, larger surrounding context for generation).

**Why it matters in delivery**

Chunking is the single most under estimated decision in RAG. Bad chunking quietly destroys retrieval quality and no amount of better models, prompts, or reranking fixes it. PMs who can spot chunking problems early save weeks of debugging.

**How it works**

| Strategy | How it works | Best for | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed size (e.g. 500 tokens) | Split text every N tokens | Quick prototypes, uniform docs | Cuts mid sentence, mid table, loses context |
| Fixed size with overlap | Split with 50 to 100 token overlap between chunks | Most production systems, good default | Still cuts structure, costs more storage |
| Structural | Split by paragraph, heading, section | Well structured docs (policies, manuals) | Requires consistent doc structure |
| Semantic | Split at topic shifts using a model | Mixed quality docs | Expensive, harder to debug |
| Hierarchical (parent child) | Small chunks for search, full sections returned | Long documents, complex Q&A | More complex pipeline |
| By format | Tables, code, headings, prose treated differently | Mixed media docs | Custom logic per format |

The most common mistake: using fixed 500 token chunks on documents where the answer to a question spans 800 tokens. The model retrieves half the answer and confidently generates the wrong half.

**Worked example**

Original policy text:

"Section 3.2 Hardship Withdrawals. A member may apply for a hardship withdrawal if they meet the following criteria: (1) Have been a member for at least 12 months; (2) Demonstrate severe financial hardship as defined in Section 3.3; (3) Have no outstanding loans against the account. Members applying must complete Form HW 100..."

Naive 50 token chunking might split mid list. A user asking "what are the hardship withdrawal requirements" might retrieve only criteria 1 and 2, generating an incomplete answer with no signal that information is missing.

Structural chunking keeps the whole section together. Answer is complete.

**PM decision points**

- Has the team inspected actual chunks to see if they make sense, or just trusted defaults?
- Are tables, lists, and code being preserved or shredded?
- Is there an eval that specifically tests retrieval recall on complex multi part questions?

**Watch outs**

- Default chunking settings in frameworks rarely fit specific documents, always inspect
- PDFs, especially scanned ones, often produce garbage chunks unless preprocessed
- Re chunking requires re embedding everything, plan for it before launch, not after

**Takeaway: chunking is where most RAG quality problems live. Inspect chunks before blaming the model.**

**Qantas story**

Chunking matters intensely for Qantas content, and naive chunking causes real problems. Consider the fare rules and conditions content that customer service teams reference dozens of times per day. A typical fare rule paragraph reads something like: "Refunds are permitted for unused tickets if cancellation occurs more than 24 hours before scheduled departure, subject to a service fee of AUD 110 per passenger per sector, unless the ticket is in a fully flexible fare class as defined in section 3.4.2, in which case no service fee applies, except for codeshare segments operated by partner airlines, where the partner's refund policy applies."

A naive 200 token chunk might capture the first part of this sentence and split before the exceptions. A customer service agent searches for "is there a refund fee on flexible fares" and the retrieval surfaces the chunk that says "subject to a service fee of AUD 110 per passenger per sector." The model confidently answers yes, charging the customer a fee that does not actually apply. The customer is upset, the agent looks unprofessional, and the airline ends up refunding the fee anyway, plus goodwill compensation.

Structural chunking preserves the full rule. The chunk boundary aligns with the policy item, not an arbitrary token count. The exception clause stays with the rule it modifies. The retrieval surfaces the complete logic and the model produces the correct answer.

Tables are the other major source of chunking pain. Qantas frequent flyer status benefits, baggage allowances, and many other content types are presented in tables. A table split across chunks loses the row column relationships. The fix is to detect tables during ingestion, keep them whole as individual chunks (with the surrounding section heading as metadata), and possibly to also generate a text serialisation of each table for additional retrieval coverage.

The deeper insight is that chunking strategy needs to reflect the structure of the source content. Different document types need different chunking rules. Fare rules are dense and exception laden, so chunks need to preserve full clauses. Crew procedures are step sequenced, so chunks should align with procedure boundaries. Engineering manuals have hierarchical structure, so chunks should carry section context. A one size fits all chunking strategy underdelivers everywhere.

For AI delivery, the practical takeaway is that the team building a RAG system needs to look at actual chunks during development and ask "would a human reading just this chunk be able to answer the kinds of questions users will ask?" If not, the chunking is wrong. This is not optional, it is the diagnostic step that catches most retrieval quality problems before launch.

**定义**

Chunking 是在 embedding 之前将文档拆分成更小片段的过程。chunk 是被检索的单位。策略范围从天真的固定大小拆分 (每 500 tokens),到结构性拆分 (按段落、章节或标题)、语义 chunking (在意义边界拆分),到分层 (用于检索的小 chunks,用于生成的较大周围上下文)。

**为何在交付中重要**

Chunking 是 RAG 中最被低估的决策。糟糕的 chunking 悄悄破坏检索质量,没有数量的更好模型、prompts 或 reranking 可以修复它。能够及早发现 chunking 问题的 PM 节省数周的调试时间。

**运作原理**

| 策略 | 它如何工作 | 最适合 | 失败模式 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 固定大小 (例如 500 tokens) | 每 N tokens 拆分文本 | 快速原型,均匀文档 | 在句子中、表格中切断,失去上下文 |
| 带重叠的固定大小 | 拆分,chunks 之间有 50 到 100 token 重叠 | 大多数生产系统,好的默认 | 仍然切断结构,成本更多存储 |
| 结构性 | 按段落、标题、章节拆分 | 良好结构化的文档 (政策、手册) | 需要一致的文档结构 |
| 语义 | 在主题转移处用模型拆分 | 质量混合的文档 | 昂贵,更难调试 |
| 分层 (父子) | 用于搜索的小 chunks,返回完整章节 | 长文档,复杂问答 | 更复杂的 pipeline |
| 按格式 | 表格、代码、标题、散文不同对待 | 混合媒体文档 | 每种格式的自定义逻辑 |

最常见的错误: 在问题答案跨越 800 tokens 的文档上使用固定的 500 token chunks。模型检索一半答案并自信地生成错误的另一半。

**举例**

原始政策文本:

"第 3.2 节困难提取。会员可以申请困难提取,如果他们满足以下标准: (1) 至少 12 个月的会员; (2) 证明第 3.3 节定义的严重财务困难; (3) 账户上没有未偿还的贷款。申请的会员必须完成表格 HW 100..."

天真的 50 token chunking 可能在列表中间拆分。问"困难提取的要求是什么"的用户可能只检索到标准 1 和 2,生成一个不完整的答案,没有信息缺失的信号。

结构性 chunking 保持整个章节在一起。答案完整。

**PM 决策点**

- 团队是否检查实际 chunks 以查看它们是否有意义,或者只是相信默认?
- 表格、列表和代码是否被保留或粉碎?
- 是否有专门测试复杂多部分问题检索召回的 eval?

**注意事项**

- 框架中的默认 chunking 设置很少适合特定文档,总是检查
- PDFs,特别是扫描的 PDF,经常产生垃圾 chunks,除非预处理
- 重新 chunking 需要重新 embedding 所有内容,在发布前计划,不是之后

**要点总结: Chunking 是大多数 RAG 质量问题存在的地方。在责怪模型之前检查 chunks。**

**Qantas 案例**

Chunking 对 Qantas 内容至关重要,天真的 chunking 造成真正的问题。考虑客户服务团队每天引用几十次的票价规则和条件内容。典型的票价规则段落读起来像这样: "如果取消发生在预定起飞前超过 24 小时,允许未使用机票的退款,需缴纳每位乘客每段 110 澳元的服务费,除非机票是第 3.4.2 节定义的完全灵活的票价类别,在这种情况下不收取服务费,但合作伙伴航空公司运营的代码共享段除外,在那里适用合作伙伴的退款政策。"

天真的 200 token chunk 可能在这句话的第一部分被捕获,并在例外之前拆分。一个客户服务代理搜索"灵活票价有退款费吗",检索浮现的 chunk 说"需缴纳每位乘客每段 110 澳元的服务费"。模型自信地回答是,向客户收取实际上不适用的费用。客户不高兴,代理看起来不专业,航空公司最终无论如何退还费用,加上善意补偿。

结构性 chunking 保留完整规则。chunk 边界与政策项目对齐,而不是任意的 token 计数。例外子句与它修改的规则保持在一起。检索浮现完整逻辑,模型产生正确答案。

表格是 chunking 痛苦的另一个主要来源。Qantas frequent flyer 状态利益、行李津贴,以及许多其他内容类型以表格呈现。跨 chunks 拆分的表格失去行列关系。修复是在摄取期间检测表格,将它们作为单个 chunks 完整保留 (将周围章节标题作为元数据),并可能为额外的检索覆盖也生成每个表格的文本序列化。

更深的洞察是 chunking 策略需要反映源内容的结构。不同的文档类型需要不同的 chunking 规则。票价规则是密集的、充满例外的,因此 chunks 需要保留完整子句。机组程序是步骤序列的,因此 chunks 应与程序边界对齐。工程手册有层次结构,因此 chunks 应携带章节上下文。一刀切的 chunking 策略到处都交付不足。

对 AI 交付来说,实践要点是构建 RAG 系统的团队需要在开发期间查看实际 chunks 并问"仅阅读这个 chunk 的人能回答用户会问的那种问题吗?"如果不能,chunking 是错的。这不是可选的,这是在发布前捕捉大多数检索质量问题的诊断步骤。
