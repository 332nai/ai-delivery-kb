**Definition**

Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) is a pattern where the system retrieves relevant information from an external knowledge source at query time and feeds it to the model as context before generating a response. The model is not trained on this data, it just reads it during the conversation.

**Why it matters in delivery**

RAG is the most common enterprise AI architecture, full stop. It solves three problems that base LLMs cannot solve alone: the model does not know your client's private data, the model's training data is months or years old, and the model has no way to cite its sources. Almost every "build a chatbot over our documents" request becomes a RAG project.

**How it works**

There are three things a base LLM cannot do that RAG fixes.

| Problem | Without RAG | With RAG |
|---|---|---|
| Private data | Model has no idea what your client's policies, contracts, or products are | Retrieves the relevant document and answers from it |
| Freshness | Model's knowledge stops at its training cutoff (often 6 to 18 months old) | Retrieves current data from a live source |
| Grounding and citations | Model generates plausible answers with no source | Returns answers tied to specific documents the user can verify |
| Cost of updates | Re training or fine tuning every time data changes | Just update the document store |
| Hallucination control | Model invents when it does not know | Model refuses or cites when retrieval returns nothing |

**PM decision points**

- Does the client's data change often (RAG) or is it stable (fine tuning could work)?
- Are citations required for compliance, audit, or trust (RAG mandatory)?
- Is the data sensitive enough that it cannot be sent to a fine tuning endpoint (RAG keeps it in the retrieval layer)?

**Watch outs**

- RAG does not magically fix hallucinations, the model can still ignore retrieved content
- "Build us a chatbot over our docs" sounds simple, but RAG quality lives or dies in the retrieval layer, not the model
- Clients often expect Google level search quality on day one, that takes real engineering

**Takeaway: RAG is the default architecture for enterprise AI. Use it when data is private, fresh, or needs to be cited.**

**Qantas story**

Qantas operates with a content estate that is exactly the shape RAG was designed for. Thousands of customer service knowledge base articles. Hundreds of policy documents covering everything from baggage to assistance dogs. Engineering manuals running to tens of thousands of pages. Crew procedures, safety bulletins, codeshare partner agreements, route specific information. None of this is in any frontier model's training data with any reliability or freshness.

Imagine the case for a unified Qantas knowledge assistant. Customer service agents need to find the right policy on a specific question quickly. Engineers need to look up procedures from manuals. Cabin crew need to verify safety procedures. Frequent Flyer team members need to navigate complex partnership rules. Today, this knowledge is scattered across multiple systems with different search interfaces, varying quality, and inconsistent maintenance.

A RAG system solves several problems at once. Knowledge stays in source systems where the content teams already manage it (no separate training process). Updates propagate when the underlying documents change (no model retraining cycle). Every answer includes citations to source documents, so the user can verify and click through. The same architecture works across multiple knowledge domains, with retrieval scoped per user or per task.

The interesting design tension at Qantas scale is around access control. Cabin crew should not see customer service compensation policies. Customer service agents should not see engineering procedures. Engineering managers should not see frequent flyer business rules. Retrieval needs to enforce access boundaries, which means the vector database has metadata for permissions, and queries are filtered before retrieval happens. This is not a feature you bolt on later, it is a fundamental architectural concern.

The other tension is content quality. RAG over good content produces good answers. RAG over outdated, inconsistent, or contradictory content produces confidently wrong answers. The single biggest predictor of RAG success at enterprise scale is the maturity of the underlying content estate, not the sophistication of the retrieval pipeline. Qantas, like many large organisations, has content quality variance across teams, and the RAG rollout would expose that variance in ways that previous search tools did not.

For AI delivery, the lesson is that RAG projects are content projects as much as they are engineering projects. The architecture is well understood. The quality outcome depends on the content. A successful enterprise RAG rollout includes a content governance workstream alongside the engineering work, and the PM who scopes it that way builds a system that earns trust over time instead of one that erodes it.

**定义**

Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) 是一种 pattern: 系统在收到查询的时候,从外部知识源检索相关信息,把信息当作上下文塞给模型,然后再生成回答。模型本身不是在这些数据上训练的,它只是在对话过程中读到了这些数据。

**为何在交付中重要**

RAG 几乎是企业 AI 最常见的架构,没有例外。它解决了基础 LLM 自己解决不了的三个问题: 模型不知道客户的私有数据;模型的训练数据是几个月、甚至几年前的;模型没办法引用自己的来源。几乎每个"在我们公司的文档上做一个聊天机器人"的请求,最后都会变成一个 RAG 项目。

**运作原理**

基础 LLM 自己做不到的三件事,RAG 都把它们补上了。

| 问题 | 没有 RAG | 有了 RAG |
|---|---|---|
| 私有数据 | 模型不知道客户的政策、合同、产品 | 检索相关文档,从文档里回答 |
| 时效性 | 模型的知识停在训练截止时间 (通常是 6 到 18 个月前) | 从实时数据源检索当前数据 |
| 来源接地和引用 | 模型给一个听起来合理的答案,没有出处 | 答案绑定到具体文档,用户可以自己验证 |
| 更新成本 | 数据变化时要重新训练或 fine tune | 更新文档存储就够了 |
| 控制幻觉 | 模型不知道的时候会编 | 检索没返回内容时,模型可以拒答或者明确引用来源 |

**PM 决策点**

- 客户的数据是频繁变化的 (RAG 是对的) 还是稳定的 (fine tuning 也许行得通)?这件事不分清,选错架构。
- 有没有合规、审计、或信任度的要求,需要给出引用? 有的话 RAG 几乎是强制的。
- 数据敏不敏感到不能送去 fine tuning 端点? 是的话,RAG 把数据留在检索层,这是更安全的设计。

**注意事项**

- RAG 不会神奇地修复幻觉。模型仍然可能无视检索回来的内容自己编。这两件事是绑在一起的。
- "在我们的文档上做个聊天机器人"听起来简单,但 RAG 的质量是死在检索那一层的,不是死在模型那一层。
- 客户经常期待第一天就有 Google 那种搜索质量。要达到那种水平,需要真功夫的工程,这一点要在 SOW 里说清楚。

**要点总结: RAG 是企业 AI 的默认架构。数据是私有的、需要时效性、或需要引用,就用 RAG。**

**Qantas 案例**

Qantas 手里的内容资产,正好就是 RAG 设计来处理的形状。几千篇客服知识库文章。几百份政策文档,覆盖从行李规则到援助犬政策的一切。几万页工程手册。机组程序、安全公告、代码共享伙伴协议、特定航线信息。这些东西没有一项是以可靠的状态或者新鲜的状态,存在于任何前沿模型的训练数据里的。

设想一个统一的 Qantas 知识助手。客服坐席需要快速找到某个具体问题的正确政策。工程师需要在手册里查程序。客舱乘务员需要核实安全流程。Frequent Flyer 团队的同事需要在复杂的合作伙伴规则里找答案。今天,这些知识散落在多个系统里,搜索界面各不相同,质量参差,维护也不一致。

一套 RAG 系统能一次性解决好几个问题。知识留在内容团队已经在管的源系统里 (不需要单独搞一套训练流程)。底层文档变化的时候,更新自然就传过去了 (不需要重新训模型)。每个答案都附上源文档的引用,用户可以自己核实、点进去看原文。同一套架构能服务多个知识域,检索按用户或任务做范围控制。

在 Qantas 这种规模上,有意思的一个设计张力是关于访问控制。客舱乘务员不该看到客服的补偿政策。客服坐席不该看到工程程序。工程经理不该看到 Frequent Flyer 的业务规则。检索这一层必须强制执行这些访问边界,这意味着 vector 数据库要带权限元数据,查询要在真正检索之前先过一道过滤。这不是后期再加的功能,这是从架构第一天就要考虑的事。

另一个张力是内容质量。在好内容上跑 RAG,你得到好答案。在过时、不一致、互相矛盾的内容上跑 RAG,你得到的是一个自信地犯错的系统。企业级 RAG 成功最大的单一预测因子,是底层内容资产的成熟度,不是检索 pipeline 的复杂度。Qantas 跟很多大组织一样,跨团队的内容质量是有差异的,RAG 推上线会以一种之前的搜索工具暴露不出来的方式,把这种差异暴露出来。

往大了说,这件事给 AI 交付的启示是: RAG 项目既是工程项目,也是内容项目。架构这块业界已经搞得很清楚了,质量结果取决于内容。一次成功的企业级 RAG 上线,要在工程工作流之外,并行跑一条内容治理工作流。把范围这样定的 PM,做出来的是一个随时间赢得信任的系统,而不是一个随时间侵蚀信任的系统。
