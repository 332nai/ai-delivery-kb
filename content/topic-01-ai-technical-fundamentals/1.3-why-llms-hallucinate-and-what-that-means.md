**Definition**

A hallucination is when a model produces output that sounds confident and coherent but is factually wrong, made up, or unsupported by the source material. It is not a bug in the traditional sense. It is a direct consequence of how LLMs work: they predict the next most plausible token, not the next true token.

**Why it matters in delivery**

Hallucinations are the single biggest reason enterprise AI pilots fail to reach production. Legal, finance, healthcare, and government clients will not deploy something that invents case law, fabricates numbers, or cites non existent policies. As a PM, you need to know that hallucinations cannot be eliminated, only reduced and managed, and you need to design the product around that fact.

**How it works**

LLMs generate text by predicting the statistically likely next token given everything before it. When the model has seen a fact many times in training, it predicts it accurately. When the fact is rare, ambiguous, or outside training data, the model still predicts something fluent, because fluency is what it was optimised for. The model has no internal "I do not know" signal unless you build one.

| Hallucination type | Example | Main fix |
|---|---|---|
| Factual fabrication | Inventing a court case or research paper | RAG with citations, source grounding |
| Numerical error | Wrong calculation in a financial summary | Tool use, call a calculator or SQL |
| Instruction drift | Ignoring "respond only in JSON" after 10 turns | Shorter context, structured output mode |
| Confident wrong answer | Stating a policy that does not exist | Retrieval plus "answer only from sources" prompt |
| Reasoning slip | Multi step logic that breaks at step 4 | Chain of thought, decomposition, verification step |

**PM decision points**

- What is the acceptable hallucination rate for this use case, and how will we measure it?
- Where in the workflow does a human verify output before it reaches a customer or regulator?
- Do we need source citations visible in the UI, and can the user click through to verify?

**Watch outs**

- "Just use a smarter model" reduces hallucinations but never eliminates them
- Confidence scores from LLMs are unreliable, do not surface them as truth signals
- Demos rarely hallucinate because the inputs are cherry picked, production traffic is messier

**Takeaway: hallucinations are a feature of how LLMs work, not a defect. Your job is to constrain, verify, and design the product so the cost of a hallucination is low.**

**Qantas story**

Aviation is one of the worst possible domains for hallucination. Consider a passenger service agent assistant asked "can a person travelling on a J class fare upgrade to first using points on this specific QF1 SYD LHR service?" The model has plausible patterns in its training data for Qantas fare classes, points programs, and route information. It can produce a confident, fluent, and entirely wrong answer in three seconds.

The actual rules involve fare bucket availability that changes by the minute, partner airline agreements that vary by sector, status tier eligibility, and aircraft type. None of these are stable enough to be in a model's training data with any reliability, and even if they were, they would be outdated. A hallucinated upgrade rule, given to an agent who then promises it to a customer, creates a service recovery problem that can run into thousands of dollars and significant brand damage.

Imagine the design pattern for a Qantas customer service AI assistant in this context. The model is not allowed to answer fare or routing questions from its own knowledge. It must retrieve current Qantas Sales Data Support content, current schedule data from the operational systems, and Frequent Flyer rule documentation. If retrieval returns nothing relevant or returns ambiguous content, the model is instructed to refuse and escalate to a human, not to attempt a fluent answer. Every output that involves a customer commitment must include the source document the answer came from.

The interesting tension is that "the AI must say I do not know" is harder to engineer than "the AI must give a confident answer." Refusal patterns are not natural to LLMs, which are trained to be helpful. They have to be designed in, evaluated for, and monitored over time.

For AI delivery, the broader insight is that hallucination is not just a quality problem, it is a liability problem. In aviation, regulated finance, healthcare, and legal services, every confident wrong answer carries real cost. The product design that works in these contexts is one where the AI's job is not to know everything, but to know what it does not know, and to surface sources for everything it does say.

**定义**

幻觉是指模型产生听起来自信、连贯,但事实错误、编造或没有源材料支持的输出。这不是传统意义上的 bug。它是 LLM 工作方式的直接后果: 它们预测下一个最合理的 token,而不是下一个真实的 token。

**为何在交付中重要**

幻觉是企业 AI 试点未能进入生产的最大单一原因。法律、金融、医疗和政府客户不会部署会编造判例法、虚构数字或引用不存在政策的系统。作为 PM,你需要知道幻觉无法消除,只能减少和管理,你需要围绕这个事实设计产品。

**运作原理**

LLM 通过预测在之前所有内容下统计上可能的下一个 token 来生成文本。当模型在训练中多次看到某个事实时,它准确地预测它。当事实罕见、模糊或在训练数据之外时,模型仍然预测某个流畅的内容,因为流畅是它被优化的目标。模型没有内部的"我不知道"信号,除非你构建一个。

| 幻觉类型 | 示例 | 主要修复方法 |
|---|---|---|
| 事实捏造 | 编造一个法庭案例或研究论文 | 带引用的 RAG,源接地 |
| 数字错误 | 财务摘要中的错误计算 | 工具使用,调用计算器或 SQL |
| 指令漂移 | 10 轮后忽略"只用 JSON 回应" | 更短的上下文,结构化输出模式 |
| 自信的错误答案 | 陈述一个不存在的政策 | 检索加"只从源回答"prompt |
| 推理疏忽 | 多步逻辑在第 4 步出错 | 思维链,分解,验证步骤 |

**PM 决策点**

- 这个用例可接受的幻觉率是多少,我们将如何衡量?
- 在到达客户或监管者之前,工作流的哪个环节有人工验证输出?
- 我们需要在 UI 中显示源引用吗,用户能点击验证吗?

**注意事项**

- "用更智能的模型"减少幻觉但从不消除
- LLM 的置信度分数不可靠,不要把它们当作真相信号呈现
- Demo 很少出现幻觉因为输入是精心挑选的,生产流量更混乱

**要点总结: 幻觉是 LLM 工作方式的特征,不是缺陷。你的工作是约束、验证,并设计产品使幻觉的成本很低。**

**Qantas 案例**

航空业是幻觉最糟糕的领域之一。设想一个客服代理助手被问到"用 J 舱票价的乘客可以用积分升级到这个特定 QF1 SYD LHR 航班的头等舱吗"。模型在训练数据中有 Qantas 票价类、积分计划和航线信息的合理模式。它能在三秒内产生一个自信、流畅,完全错误的答案。

实际规则涉及按分钟变化的票价桶可用性、按航段不同的合作伙伴航空公司协议、状态等级资格,以及飞机类型。这些没有一个稳定到足以在模型的训练数据中可靠存在,即使存在也会过时。一个被代理人后续承诺给客户的虚构升级规则,造成的服务恢复问题可能达到数千美元,以及显著的品牌损害。

设想 Qantas 客户服务 AI 助手在这种情况下的设计模式。模型不被允许从自己的知识回答票价或路线问题。它必须检索当前的 Qantas Sales Data Support 内容、运营系统的当前时刻表数据,以及 Frequent Flyer 规则文档。如果检索什么都没返回,或返回模糊内容,模型被指示拒绝并升级给人工,而不是尝试给出流畅的答案。每个涉及客户承诺的输出必须包含答案来源文档。

有趣的张力在于,"AI 必须说我不知道"比"AI 必须给出自信的答案"更难工程化。拒绝模式对 LLM 来说不是自然的,它们被训练成提供帮助。它们必须被设计进来,被评估,并随时间监控。

对 AI 交付来说,更广泛的洞察是幻觉不仅仅是质量问题,它是责任问题。在航空业、监管严格的金融业、医疗业和法律服务业,每一个自信的错误答案都带有真实成本。在这些情境中可行的产品设计,是让 AI 的工作不是无所不知,而是知道它不知道什么,并为它说的每一件事都提供来源。
