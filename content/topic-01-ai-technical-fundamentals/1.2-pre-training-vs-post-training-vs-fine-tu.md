**Definition**

Pre training is where a model learns language and world knowledge by reading trillions of tokens of internet and book text. Post training is where the lab shapes that raw model into something helpful, safe, and conversational using human feedback and curated examples. Fine tuning is when you, the customer, take an already trained model and nudge it further on your own data for your specific use case.

**Why it matters in delivery**

Clients constantly confuse these three. They say "we want to train the model on our data" when they almost always mean fine tuning, and even then, they usually do not need it. Getting this vocabulary right saves weeks of misaligned scoping and prevents you from quoting a six figure training project when prompting plus RAG would have worked.

**How it works**

Think of it as three stages of building a junior consultant.

| Stage | Who does it | What happens | Analogy | Time and cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre training | Frontier lab only | Model reads the internet, learns patterns of language and facts | University degree, 4 years of general study | Months, hundreds of millions of USD |
| Post training | Frontier lab only | Model learns to follow instructions, be helpful, refuse harmful requests, via SFT, RLHF, DPO | Graduate program at the consulting firm | Weeks, millions of USD |
| Fine tuning | You, the customer | Existing model adapted on your domain data or tone | A week of onboarding to a specific client account | Hours to days, hundreds to low thousands of USD |

You cannot do pre training. You almost never need to do post training. You can do fine tuning, but usually should not start there.

**PM decision points**

- Is the client actually asking for fine tuning, or do they mean "make it know our stuff"?
- Have we exhausted prompting and RAG before recommending fine tuning?
- If we fine tune, do we have at least 500 to 1,000 high quality labelled examples?

**Watch outs**

- Fine tuning locks you to a model version, so upgrades become a re training cost
- Fine tuning teaches style and format well, but does not reliably teach new facts, RAG does that
- Clients hear "training" and assume their data improves the base model for everyone, clarify that this is not how API fine tuning works

**Takeaway: you cannot pre train, you do not need to post train, and you probably should not fine tune yet. Start with prompting and retrieval.**

**Qantas story**

When the Qantas data and AI team first started exploring generative AI in 2023, executive sponsors latched onto the idea of "training Qantas's own model" on decades of operational data, customer interactions, and engineering manuals. The internal narrative was that Qantas had a proprietary data advantage and should "build a Qantas AI." This is a remarkably common pattern at enterprises with strong brand identity.

The reality, once technical teams unpacked it, was very different. Pre training was financially absurd for an airline (hundreds of millions of USD, the kind of investment that builds new aircraft, not chatbots). Post training was the wrong tool because Qantas did not need a generally capable assistant from scratch, it needed task specific behaviour. The actual technical need across most pilot use cases (customer service, engineering Q&A, marketing copy, internal knowledge search) was prompting with examples plus retrieval over Qantas content.

A more focused exploration of fine tuning was warranted for one narrow category: matching the specific tone and structure of Qantas customer service responses, where consistency mattered and prompting alone produced variable results. Even there, the team built a strong prompted baseline first and only considered fine tuning for the residual gap.

For AI delivery, the takeaway is to watch for the "train our own model" narrative in client conversations. It often signals strategic intent (we want to own our AI capability) more than technical need (we need a different model behaviour). The right delivery response is to acknowledge the strategic intent and translate it into a realistic technical roadmap. Most of the time, that roadmap is prompting and retrieval over the client's data, with fine tuning reserved for the narrow cases where it materially closes a remaining gap. Mislabelling those three stages costs months and credibility.

**定义**

Pre training 是模型通过阅读数万亿 tokens 的互联网和书籍文本学习语言和世界知识的过程。Post training 是实验室使用人类反馈和精选示例,将原始模型塑造成有帮助、安全、可对话的产品。Fine tuning 是你作为客户,把已经训练好的模型,基于你自己的数据进一步调整,用于特定用例。

**为何在交付中重要**

客户经常把这三个混淆。他们说"我们想用我们的数据训练模型"时,几乎总是指 fine tuning,即便如此,他们通常也不需要。把这个词汇搞对可以节省数周错位的需求确定时间,避免你在 prompt 加 RAG 就能解决的情况下报价六位数的训练项目。

**运作原理**

把它想象成构建初级顾问的三个阶段。

| 阶段 | 谁来做 | 发生了什么 | 类比 | 时间和成本 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre training | 仅前沿实验室 | 模型阅读互联网,学习语言和事实的模式 | 大学学位,4 年通识学习 | 数月,数亿美元 |
| Post training | 仅前沿实验室 | 模型学习遵循指令、提供帮助、拒绝有害请求,通过 SFT、RLHF、DPO | 咨询公司的研究生项目 | 数周,数百万美元 |
| Fine tuning | 你,作为客户 | 现有模型在你的领域数据或语气上进行调整 | 针对特定客户账户一周的入职培训 | 数小时到数天,数百到几千美元 |

你做不了 pre training。你几乎从不需要做 post training。你可以做 fine tuning,但通常不应从这里开始。

**PM 决策点**

- 客户是真的在要求 fine tuning,还是他们指"让它了解我们的东西"?
- 在推荐 fine tuning 之前,我们已经穷尽了 prompting 和 RAG 吗?
- 如果我们做 fine tuning,我们有至少 500 到 1,000 个高质量的标注示例吗?

**注意事项**

- Fine tuning 把你锁定到一个模型版本,所以升级变成重新训练的成本
- Fine tuning 能很好地教授风格和格式,但无法可靠地教授新事实,RAG 才能做到
- 客户听到"训练"会假设他们的数据改进了所有人的基础模型,要澄清这不是 API fine tuning 的工作方式

**要点总结: 你做不了 pre training,你不需要做 post training,你可能还不应该做 fine tuning。从 prompting 和检索开始。**

**Qantas 案例**

当 Qantas 数据和 AI 团队在 2023 年首次开始探索生成式 AI 时,高层赞助人抓住了"训练 Qantas 自己的模型"的想法,用几十年的运营数据、客户互动和工程手册。内部的叙事是 Qantas 有专有数据优势,应该"构建一个 Qantas AI"。这是在品牌识别度强的企业中非常常见的模式。

一旦技术团队拆解开来,现实非常不同。Pre training 对航空公司来说在财务上是荒谬的 (数亿美元,这种投资是用来造新飞机的,不是聊天机器人)。Post training 是错误的工具,因为 Qantas 不需要从头构建一个通用能力的助手,它需要任务特定的行为。大多数试点用例 (客户服务、工程问答、营销文案、内部知识搜索) 的实际技术需求是 prompting 加示例,再加上对 Qantas 内容的检索。

对一类狭窄的用例,更聚焦的 fine tuning 探索是合理的: 匹配 Qantas 客户服务响应的特定语气和结构,在这里一致性很重要,单独 prompting 产生的结果不稳定。即便在那里,团队也先构建了一个强 prompt 基线,只有在残余差距上才考虑 fine tuning。

对 AI 交付来说,启示是要警惕客户对话中的"训练我们自己的模型"叙事。它通常更多地表明战略意图 (我们想拥有自己的 AI 能力) 而非技术需求 (我们需要不同的模型行为)。正确的交付响应是承认战略意图并将其转化为现实的技术路线图。大多数时候,该路线图是在客户数据上做 prompting 和检索,fine tuning 仅用于实质性缩小残余差距的狭窄情形。把这三个阶段贴错标签会损失数月时间和信誉。
