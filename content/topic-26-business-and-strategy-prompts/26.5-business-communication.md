# Business Communication

30 prompts for difficult conversations, negotiation, and executive comms.

## Difficult conversations

1. Help me prep for telling [person] that [bad news]. List 3 framings.
2. Draft a script to give [direct report] feedback on [issue].
3. Write what to say to push back on [request from boss] without damage.
4. Help me say no to [request] while keeping the door open.
5. Draft a message escalating [issue] to [senior] without blame.
6. Help me apologize for [mistake] without overdoing it.
7. Write a script for asking for a raise based on [accomplishments].
8. Draft how to deliver [criticism] to a peer who's defensive.
9. Write a message resetting expectations with [stakeholder] on [topic].
10. Help me write a thank-you to [person] who shipped under pressure.

## Negotiation and persuasion

11. Identify [counterparty]'s BATNA, likely opening, and probable concessions.
12. Draft 3 framings of [proposal] for an audience that's [skeptical/eager].
13. Generate 5 questions to ask in a negotiation to surface hidden interests.
14. Write objection-handling responses to the 5 most likely pushbacks on [proposal].
15. Help me make a counteroffer to [offer] that anchors [our position].
16. Identify what [counterparty] cares about beyond price/scope in [deal].
17. Draft a walk-away message that leaves the door open for [deal].
18. Build a 'multiple equivalent simultaneous offer' (MESO) for [negotiation].
19. Generate persuasive framings using loss aversion for [pitch].
20. Write a 'why now' argument for a deal that's been sitting in [stage].

## Executive communication

21. Convert my long email to the CEO into 3 bullets and one ask: [paste].
22. Draft a one-paragraph briefing for [exec] before [meeting].
23. Write a 'how do I want this exec to remember me?' opening for [meeting].
24. Translate this technical update into exec-friendly language: [paste].
25. Generate 3 questions [CFO/CTO/CEO] is likely to ask about [project].
26. Draft a board update for [project] — 1 slide, headline + 3 numbers.
27. Write a 'what I want from you' ask for a 15-min exec meeting on [topic].
28. Draft a follow-up note 24 hours after a meeting with [exec].
29. Convert this 5-page memo into a 1-page exec summary: [paste].
30. Suggest the right channel (email/meeting/slack/doc) for [communication].

**Total prompts: 30**

# 商务沟通

30 条 prompt: 难谈话、谈判、对高管沟通。

## 难谈话

1. 帮我准备告诉 [人] [坏消息]。列 3 种定调。
2. 起草一份脚本,给 [下属] 关于 [问题] 的反馈。
3. 写一份对 [上级请求] 推回但不伤关系的措辞。
4. 帮我对 [请求] 说不,但留余地。
5. 起草一条把 [问题] 升级到 [资深人] 的消息,不指责。
6. 帮我对 [失误] 道歉,但不过度。
7. 基于 [成绩] 写一份要求加薪的脚本。
8. 起草怎么把 [批评] 给一个易防御的同事。
9. 写一条消息,跟 [干系人] 在 [话题] 上重置预期。
10. 帮我给 [人] 写一份感谢,他在压力下完成了任务。

## 谈判与说服

11. 识别 [对方] 的 BATNA、可能的开局、可能的让步。
12. 为 [怀疑/积极] 的受众起草 [提议] 的 3 种定调。
13. 在谈判中拟定 5 个能挖出潜在利益的问题。
14. 为 [提议] 的 5 个最可能反驳写应对话术。
15. 帮我对 [报价] 做出能锚定 [我方立场] 的还价。
16. 识别 [对方] 在 [交易] 里除价格/范围外在意什么。
17. 起草一份离场措辞,但为 [交易] 留下回旋余地。
18. 为 [谈判] 构建一组同时等价的多备选 (MESO)。
19. 为 [推介] 用损失厌恶生成有说服力的定调。
20. 为停留在 [阶段] 的交易写一段'为什么现在'。

## 高管沟通

21. 把我给 CEO 的长邮件改成 3 个要点 + 一个请求: [粘贴]。
22. 为 [高管] 在 [会议] 前起草一段简报。
23. 为 [会议] 写一个'我希望这位高管怎么记住我'的开场。
24. 把这条技术更新翻译成高管友好的语言: [粘贴]。
25. 生成 [CFO/CTO/CEO] 对 [项目] 可能问的 3 个问题。
26. 为 [项目] 起草一页董事会更新 — 标题 + 3 个数字。
27. 为 [话题] 15 分钟高管会议写一段'我希望从你这拿走什么'的请求。
28. 在与 [高管] 会后 24 小时内起草一份跟进笔记。
29. 把这份 5 页备忘录改成 1 页高管摘要: [粘贴]。
30. 为 [沟通] 建议合适的渠道 (邮件/会议/Slack/文档)。

**共 30 条 prompt**
