# Projects and Process

30 prompts for planning, scoping, status, and process improvement.

## Planning and scoping

1. Break down [project] into milestones with deadlines and owners.
2. Identify 5 dependencies and risks for [project] in week one.
3. Draft a one-page project charter for [initiative].
4. Write a RACI matrix for [project] with [N] roles and [N] tasks.
5. List the 'will do' and 'will not do' for v1 of [project].
6. Estimate effort (T-shirt size) for these 10 tasks: [paste].
7. Propose a 3-phase rollout plan for [feature/product].
8. Draft success criteria for [project] — 5 specific, measurable outcomes.
9. Identify 3 ways [project] could be over-scoped, suggest cuts.
10. Sketch a critical path for [project] with [N] milestones.

## Status and tracking

11. Write a weekly status update for [project]: progress, blockers, asks.
12. Draft a RAG status assessment for [project] — red/amber/green and why.
13. Summarize 6 months of [project] for a stakeholder who's been absent.
14. Identify 3 leading indicators that [project] is going off track.
15. Write a delay announcement for [milestone] that doesn't lose trust.
16. Draft a 'project health' dashboard layout — what 5 things to show.
17. Generate 5 questions to ask team in a status check-in.
18. Write a stakeholder map for [project] with their info needs.
19. Convert this update from active-tense to risk-aware language: [paste].
20. Detect over-optimism in this status report — what would a skeptic see? [paste].

## Process improvement

21. Audit our [process] and suggest 3 simplifications.
22. Identify the bottleneck in [workflow]: [describe steps].
23. Compare our process to industry best practice for [type of work].
24. Design a feedback loop for [team/process] that catches issues early.
25. Write a SOP for [recurring task] that a new hire can follow.
26. Propose 3 automation candidates in [team's workflow].
27. Identify low-value meetings in [team's calendar] and propose alternatives.
28. Design a quarterly retro template for [team type].
29. Write a 'what we stopped doing' list for [team] this quarter.
30. Map [team]'s capacity vs demand — flag 3 systemic mismatches.

**Total prompts: 30**

# 项目与流程

30 条 prompt: 规划、范围、状态、流程改进。

## 规划与范围

1. 把 [项目] 拆成里程碑,带截止日期和负责人。
2. 在 [项目] 第一周识别 5 个依赖和风险。
3. 为 [行动] 起草一页项目宪章。
4. 为 [项目] 写 RACI 矩阵,[N] 个角色和 [N] 个任务。
5. 列出 [项目] v1 的'要做'和'不做'清单。
6. 对这 10 个任务做估算 (T 恤衫大小): [粘贴]。
7. 为 [功能/产品] 提议 3 阶段上线计划。
8. 为 [项目] 起草成功标准 — 5 个具体可量化的结果。
9. 识别 [项目] 可能被超范围的 3 处,建议砍点。
10. 为有 [N] 个里程碑的 [项目] 画关键路径。

## 状态与追踪

11. 为 [项目] 写一份周报: 进展、阻碍、请求。
12. 为 [项目] 起草红黄绿状态评估,说明为什么。
13. 为缺席的干系人总结 [项目] 过去 6 个月。
14. 识别 [项目] 偏轨的 3 个先行指标。
15. 为 [里程碑] 写一份不丢信任的延期通知。
16. 起草项目健康仪表盘布局 — 该展示哪 5 件事。
17. 为状态校准会拟定向团队提的 5 个问题。
18. 为 [项目] 画干系人地图,标出各自的信息需求。
19. 把这份更新从主动语态改成风险敏感语气: [粘贴]。
20. 在这份状态报告里查找过度乐观 — 怀疑者会看到什么? [粘贴]。

## 流程改进

21. 审视我们的 [流程],建议 3 处简化。
22. 识别 [工作流] 的瓶颈: [描述步骤]。
23. 把我们的流程对照行业最佳实践 [工作类型]。
24. 为 [团队/流程] 设计一个能尽早发现问题的反馈回路。
25. 为 [周期性任务] 写一份新人能照做的 SOP。
26. 在 [团队工作流] 中提议 3 个可自动化的候选。
27. 在 [团队日程] 里识别低价值会议并提议替代。
28. 为 [团队类型] 设计一个季度复盘模板。
29. 为 [团队] 本季度写一份'我们停止做的事'清单。
30. 画 [团队] 产能对需求图 — 标出 3 处系统性失衡。

**共 30 条 prompt**
