**Definition**

There are two main families of fine tuning that customers can apply. Supervised fine tuning (SFT) trains the model on input output pairs, showing it the right answer for each input. Preference tuning (RLHF, DPO) trains the model on comparisons, showing it which of two responses is better. These are the same techniques the labs use during post training, applied to your specific data.

**Why it matters in delivery**

Clients ask "can we fine tune?" without knowing what kind. The two types solve different problems. SFT teaches the model what to produce. Preference tuning teaches the model what to prefer. Knowing the difference lets you scope realistically and explain trade offs to the client.

**How it works**

| Method | What it teaches | Data needed | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supervised fine tuning (SFT) | "For this input, produce this output" | 500 to 10,000+ labelled examples | Moderate, well supported in APIs |
| RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) | Prefer responses humans rate higher | Thousands of preference comparisons plus reward model | Heavy, rarely done by customers |
| DPO (Direct Preference Optimisation) | Prefer responses humans rate higher, simpler than RLHF | Pairs of preferred vs rejected responses | Moderate, increasingly available |
| Constitutional / rule based | Prefer responses that follow rules | Rule set plus pairs | Specialised, lab grade |

For most enterprise customers, SFT is the only realistic option. Preference tuning is available via some APIs but requires preference data most teams do not have.

**Worked example**

SFT data looks like:

```
Input: "Summarise this medical record for the GP referral letter"
Output: "[example summary in the exact format the client wants]"
```

You provide 1,000 examples like this. The model learns the pattern.

DPO data looks like:

```
Input: "Draft a renewal email to a frustrated customer"
Chosen response: "[good response]"
Rejected response: "[mediocre response]"
```

You provide 500 pairs. The model learns to prefer the chosen style.

**PM decision points**

- Does the client have the right kind of data, labelled pairs for SFT, preference comparisons for DPO?
- Is the goal to teach a specific output (SFT) or to nudge style (preference)?
- Is preference tuning even available on the chosen model, many APIs only support SFT?

**Watch outs**

- SFT requires high quality examples, garbage in garbage out is amplified
- Preference tuning needs preference data, which is expensive to collect well
- Both methods need an eval set the fine tuning data has never seen, otherwise you cannot measure generalisation

**Takeaway: SFT is the realistic fine tuning option for most enterprise projects. Preference tuning exists but rarely fits client data and budget.**

**Qantas story**

The two main fine tuning approaches available to Qantas would be supervised fine tuning (SFT) and direct preference optimisation (DPO), and the choice depends on what kind of data the team actually has.

SFT works when there is a corpus of input output pairs that represent the desired behaviour. For example, the Qantas customer communications team has thousands of historical delay notifications they have written over the years, each paired with the underlying flight disruption details. That is exactly the right shape for SFT. The team could fine tune a smaller model on these pairs and end up with a model that produces notifications in the precise Qantas voice, with consistent structure, appropriate tone, and the specific phrasings the brand team prefers. The cost is moderate, the data is already labelled, and the result is a specialist model for that task.

DPO requires preference data, pairs of outputs where humans have indicated which one is better. This is much harder to produce. Most Qantas teams have outputs that were produced (the historical delay notifications) but no parallel set of rejected alternatives. Creating preference data retroactively means generating multiple candidate outputs, having brand or content reviewers grade them, and assembling pairs. This is expensive enough that DPO is rarely the right approach for an enterprise team that does not already have preference data flowing from another source.

The more interesting practical pattern at Qantas would be to fine tune small models with SFT for high volume narrow tasks, leaving the flagship API model for hard general tasks. A fine tuned 8B model on customer service classification, running on Bedrock with provisioned throughput, can handle millions of classifications per month at a cost that is dramatically lower than flagship API consumption. The classification accuracy can be tuned to within a percentage point or two of flagship quality on the specific task. The savings are real and the engineering is well understood.

The cases where this does not work are also worth knowing. Fine tuning teaches style, format, and narrow task behaviour. It does not reliably teach new facts. If the goal is for the model to "know about Qantas products and policies," fine tuning is the wrong tool. RAG over current product documentation is the right tool. Mixing these up is a common scoping mistake.

For AI delivery, the lesson is that fine tuning is a real tool in the toolbox for specific, well defined situations. The discipline is to know what shape of data you have, what kind of behaviour you are trying to teach, and whether the task and volume justify the engineering. Most enterprise teams ask for fine tuning because it sounds sophisticated and end up not needing it. The minority who genuinely need it benefit from doing it well, with proper eval discipline.

**定义**

客户可以应用的两种主要 fine tuning 家族。监督 fine tuning (SFT) 在输入输出对上训练模型,显示每个输入的正确答案。偏好调整 (RLHF、DPO) 在比较上训练模型,显示两个响应中哪个更好。这些是实验室在 post training 期间使用的相同技术,应用于你的特定数据。

**为何在交付中重要**

客户问"我们可以 fine tune 吗",不知道是哪种。两种类型解决不同的问题。SFT 教模型生成什么。偏好调整教模型偏好什么。了解差异让你能够实际地确定范围,并向客户解释权衡。

**运作原理**

| 方法 | 它教什么 | 需要的数据 | 努力 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 监督 fine tuning (SFT) | "对于这个输入,产生这个输出" | 500 到 10,000+ 个标注示例 | 中等,API 中良好支持 |
| RLHF (从人类反馈强化学习) | 偏好人类评分更高的响应 | 数千个偏好比较加奖励模型 | 重,客户很少做 |
| DPO (直接偏好优化) | 偏好人类评分更高的响应,比 RLHF 简单 | 偏好响应与拒绝响应的对 | 中等,越来越可用 |
| 宪法 / 基于规则 | 偏好遵循规则的响应 | 规则集加对 | 专业化,实验室级别 |

对大多数企业客户,SFT 是唯一现实的选项。偏好调整通过一些 API 可用,但需要大多数团队没有的偏好数据。

**举例**

SFT 数据看起来像:

```
输入: "总结这份医疗记录用于 GP 推荐信"
输出: "[精确格式中客户想要的示例摘要]"
```

你提供 1,000 个这样的示例。模型学习模式。

DPO 数据看起来像:

```
输入: "为沮丧的客户起草续约电子邮件"
选择的响应: "[好的响应]"
被拒绝的响应: "[平庸的响应]"
```

你提供 500 个对。模型学会偏好选择的风格。

**PM 决策点**

- 客户是否有正确类型的数据,SFT 的标注对、DPO 的偏好比较?
- 目标是教特定输出 (SFT) 还是微调风格 (偏好)?
- 偏好调整在所选模型上是否可用,许多 API 仅支持 SFT?

**注意事项**

- SFT 需要高质量示例,垃圾进垃圾出被放大
- 偏好调整需要偏好数据,这很贵难以收集得好
- 两种方法都需要 fine tuning 数据从未见过的 eval 集,否则你无法衡量泛化

**要点总结: SFT 是大多数企业项目现实的 fine tuning 选项。偏好调整存在,但很少适合客户数据和预算。**

**Qantas 案例**

可供 Qantas 的两种主要 fine tuning 方法将是监督 fine tuning (SFT) 和直接偏好优化 (DPO),选择取决于团队实际拥有什么样的数据。

SFT 在有代表所需行为的输入输出对语料库时工作。例如,Qantas 客户沟通团队多年来写了数千条历史延误通知,每条都与底层航班中断详情配对。那正是 SFT 的正确形状。团队可以在这些对上 fine tune 较小的模型,最终得到一个以精确 Qantas 声音、一致结构、适当语气和品牌团队偏好的特定措辞产生通知的模型。成本适中,数据已标注,结果是该任务的专家模型。

DPO 需要偏好数据,人类已经指出哪个更好的输出对。这要难得多产生。大多数 Qantas 团队有产生的输出 (历史延误通知),但没有平行的拒绝替代方案集。回顾性地创建偏好数据意味着生成多个候选输出、让品牌或内容审查员评分,并组装对。这足够贵,以至于 DPO 很少是不已经从其他来源流入偏好数据的企业团队的正确方法。

Qantas 更有趣的实际模式将是用 SFT fine tune 小型模型用于高量窄任务,将旗舰 API 模型留给困难的通用任务。在客户服务分类上 fine tuned 的 8B 模型,在 Bedrock 上运行带预配吞吐量,可以以比旗舰 API 消费显著低得多的成本每月处理数百万分类。分类准确性可以调到具体任务上旗舰质量的一两个百分点内。节省是真实的,工程是众所周知的。

这不起作用的案例也值得了解。Fine tuning 教风格、格式和窄任务行为。它不可靠地教新事实。如果目标是让模型"了解 Qantas 产品和政策",fine tuning 是错误的工具。在当前产品文档上的 RAG 是正确的工具。混淆这些是常见的需求确定错误。

对 AI 交付来说,启示是 fine tuning 是工具箱中针对特定、定义良好情况的真正工具。纪律是知道你有什么形状的数据、你试图教什么样的行为,以及任务和量是否证明工程合理。大多数企业团队要求 fine tuning,因为它听起来复杂,最终发现不需要。真正需要它的少数受益于做得好,带适当的 eval 纪律。
