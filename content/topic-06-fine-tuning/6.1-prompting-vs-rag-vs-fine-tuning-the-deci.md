**Definition**

These are the three main ways to make an LLM behave the way you want for a specific use case. Prompting changes the instructions you send to the model. RAG provides external information at query time. Fine tuning modifies the model itself by training it on your data. They solve different problems and are often used together, but the order matters.

**Why it matters in delivery**

Picking the wrong one wastes weeks and tens of thousands of dollars. The most common mistake is jumping to fine tuning when prompting or RAG would have worked. The second most common mistake is using RAG when prompting alone was enough. PMs who know the decision tree make defensible architecture calls quickly.

**How it works**

The simple decision sequence:

1. Try prompting first. If the model can do it with the right instructions and examples, stop.
2. If the model lacks knowledge (your data, recent data), add RAG.
3. If the model still cannot reliably produce the right format, tone, or behaviour after good prompting, then consider fine tuning.

{{chart: prompt-rag-finetune-tree}}

| Approach | Solves | Does not solve | Effort | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prompting | Behaviour, format, tone, simple task framing | Lack of knowledge, very specialised domains | Hours | Just inference cost |
| RAG | Private data, fresh data, citations, large knowledge bases | Style consistency, format reliability, latency | Days to weeks | Inference plus retrieval infra |
| Fine tuning | Style, format consistency, narrow specialised tasks, smaller model matching larger | Knowledge gaps (RAG does that better), rapidly changing data | Weeks | Training plus higher inference cost |

**Worked example**

Client request: "Make the model answer support questions like our top agent Sarah."

Wrong path: jump to fine tuning on Sarah's transcripts. Three months, USD 80K, mediocre results.

Right path:

1. Prompting: "You are a customer support agent. Tone is warm but direct. Always confirm understanding before answering. Here are 5 examples of Sarah's style." Test. Often gets 80 percent of the way.
2. RAG: add Sarah's knowledge base, product docs, past tickets. Gets to 95 percent.
3. Only then consider fine tuning if the remaining 5 percent is worth the cost.

**PM decision points**

- Has the team genuinely exhausted prompting before recommending anything more complex?
- Is the gap a knowledge problem (RAG) or a behaviour problem (fine tuning)?
- Can the client articulate what fine tuning would deliver that prompting plus RAG cannot?

**Watch outs**

- Clients hear "fine tuning" and assume it is the sophisticated answer, it is usually not
- Fine tuning locks you to a model version, every upgrade costs another training cycle
- The decision should be data driven, build an eval first, then test each approach against it

**Takeaway: prompt first, retrieve second, fine tune last. The default sequence saves most projects from over engineering.**

**Qantas story**

Within Qantas, the decision tree between prompting, RAG, and fine tuning plays out across many use cases, and the most expensive mistakes happen when teams jump to fine tuning too early.

Consider a hypothetical use case: generating Qantas brand consistent marketing copy for route specific promotions. The naive scoping says "fine tune a model on our existing marketing copy so it sounds like us." The budget is AUD 80K and the timeline is three months.

A disciplined scoping process tests each step of the decision tree first. Step one, prompting. A strong system prompt with five example pieces of Qantas marketing copy, clear tone guidelines (warm, optimistic, never overpromising), and the specific route context produces output that hits the brand voice for the vast majority of cases. Test with editors, get qualitative feedback, refine the system prompt.

Step two, RAG. For copy that references specific Qantas content (route history, frequent flyer benefits, destination experiences), add retrieval over the Qantas marketing content library. This grounds claims in actual brand approved language and prevents hallucinated facts about routes or services.

After steps one and two, evaluate honestly. If the output meets the quality bar, ship it. If there is a residual gap, characterise it. Is it a brand voice issue (more example tuning might fix it), a knowledge issue (better retrieval would fix it), or a format issue (fine tuning might fix it)?

In most cases like this, fine tuning is unnecessary. The combination of strong prompting and good retrieval produces output indistinguishable from brand approved copy. The AUD 80K fine tuning budget is redirected to evaluation infrastructure, content governance for the retrieval source, and ongoing prompt refinement. The timeline shrinks from three months to four weeks.

The cases where fine tuning genuinely earns its place at Qantas would be narrower. One example might be matching a very specific format like operational notices to crew, where the structure is rigid, the vocabulary is specialised, and the volume justifies the engineering. Even there, the right starting point is exhausted prompting plus RAG, and fine tuning is the last resort, not the first.

For AI delivery, the broader insight is that the decision tree exists precisely because skipping it is so common and so expensive. Most enterprise AI projects that "need fine tuning" actually need better prompting and retrieval. The delivery practitioner who can walk a client through the tree calmly, with eval data at each step, saves the client real money and ships faster. The client appreciates this, and it sets up future projects to be scoped the same way.

**定义**

这是让 LLM 在特定用例上按你想要的方式表现的三种主要方法。Prompting 改的是你发给模型的指令。RAG 在查询的时候把外部信息塞进去。Fine tuning 通过在你的数据上训练,直接修改模型本身。它们解决的是不同的问题,而且经常会一起用,但用的顺序很重要。

**为何在交付中重要**

选错路浪费的是几周时间和几万澳元。最常见的错误是: 在 prompting 或 RAG 本来就能解决的时候,跳去做 fine tuning。第二常见的错误是: 在只用 prompting 就够的时候,硬上 RAG。能把这棵决策树搞清楚的 PM,能快速做出经得起追问的架构决策。

**运作原理**

简单的决策顺序:

1. 先试 prompting。如果一个好指令加上几个示例,模型就能做对,停。
2. 如果模型缺的是知识 (你的私有数据、近期数据),加 RAG。
3. 如果在 prompting 写得够好的前提下,模型在格式、语气、行为上还是不可靠,那时候再考虑 fine tuning。

{{chart: prompt-rag-finetune-tree}}

| 方法 | 能解决 | 解决不了 | 工作量 | 成本 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prompting | 行为、格式、语气、简单任务的框架 | 知识缺失、非常专业的领域 | 几小时 | 只有推理成本 |
| RAG | 私有数据、最新数据、引用、大型知识库 | 风格一致性、格式可靠性、延迟 | 几天到几周 | 推理 + 检索基础设施 |
| Fine tuning | 风格、格式一致性、窄场景专业任务、让小模型达到接近大模型的水平 | 知识缺口 (RAG 更合适)、变化快的数据 | 几周 | 训练 + 推理成本更高 |

**举例**

客户请求: "让模型像我们最好的客服 Sarah 那样回答支持问题。"

错的路径: 直接拿 Sarah 的工单对话去做 fine tuning。三个月、8 万澳元、结果平庸。

对的路径:

1. Prompting: "你是一名客服坐席。语气温暖但直接。回答前永远先确认你理解了用户的问题。这里有 5 个 Sarah 风格的示例。" 跑测试。通常 80% 的水平就达到了。
2. RAG: 加上 Sarah 写过的 KB、产品文档、历史工单。能上到 95%。
3. 只有到这一步,如果剩下的 5% 真的值这个成本,才考虑 fine tuning。

**PM 决策点**

- 团队真的把 prompting 这条路走到尽头了吗?在推荐任何更复杂的方案之前,这件事要先确认。
- 这个差距是知识问题 (走 RAG),还是行为问题 (才有可能走 fine tuning)?这两件事经常被搞混。
- 客户能不能讲清楚 fine tuning 能交付出什么,是 prompting + RAG 做不到的?讲不清的话,先不动 fine tuning。

**注意事项**

- 客户听到"fine tuning"会以为这是一个高级答案。通常它不是。
- Fine tuning 会把你锁在某一个模型版本上。每次升级都要再做一轮训练,这是隐形成本。
- 决策要靠数据。先搭好 eval,再分别测每条路径的表现,不要靠直觉做选型。

**要点总结: 先 prompt,再 retrieve,最后才 fine tune。这个默认顺序能把大部分项目从过度工程里救出来。**

**Qantas 案例**

在 Qantas 内部,prompting、RAG、fine tuning 之间的决策树在很多用例上一遍遍上演,最贵的错误都发生在团队太早跳到 fine tuning 这一步。

设想一个假设的 Qantas 团队: 给特定航线促销生成 Qantas 品牌调性一致的营销文案。天真的需求确定说: "拿我们现有的营销文案去 fine tune 一个模型,让它听起来像我们。" 预算 8 万澳元,时间线三个月。

有纪律的需求确定流程,先把决策树每一步都测一遍。第一步,prompting。一份强 system prompt,带 5 篇 Qantas 营销文案示例、清晰的语气指南 (温暖、乐观、绝不过度承诺)、外加具体航线的上下文,产出的东西在绝大多数情况下已经命中品牌调性。让编辑测一下,拿定性反馈,迭代 system prompt。

第二步,RAG。对那些要引用具体 Qantas 内容 (航线历史、Frequent Flyer 权益、目的地体验) 的文案,加上对 Qantas 营销内容库的检索。这一步把所有声明都锚到品牌批准过的真实内容上,防止编出航线或服务的虚假事实。

走完第一步和第二步,诚实评估。输出达到质量门槛,就发。还有差距,把它描述清楚: 这是品牌语气问题 (再调几个示例可能就好)、知识问题 (改 RAG 就能修)、还是格式问题 (这种才可能需要 fine tuning)?

绝大多数像这样的案例,fine tuning 是不必要的。强 prompting 加好的检索,出来的内容跟品牌批准过的文案没法区分。本来那 8 万澳元的 fine tuning 预算,转去投资 eval 基础设施、检索源的内容治理、以及持续的 prompt 调优。时间线从三个月缩到四周。

Fine tuning 在 Qantas 真正能赢得自己位置的场景更窄。一个例子可能是: 匹配像"给机组的运营通告"这种非常特定的格式——结构严格、词汇专业、量大到能撑起工程投入。但即便在那个场景下,正确的起点也是先把 prompting + RAG 走到极限,fine tuning 是最后手段,不是第一选择。

往大了说,这件事给 AI 交付的启示是: 这棵决策树之所以存在,正是因为"跳过它"这件事既常见又昂贵。大部分号称"需要 fine tuning"的企业 AI 项目,真正需要的是更好的 prompting 和检索。一个能冷静带客户走完这棵树、在每一步都带着 eval 数据的交付者,能给客户省下真金白银,而且发得更快。客户会真心欣赏这种做法,而且这一次的合作会为后面的项目立下"也按这套来定范围"的先例。
