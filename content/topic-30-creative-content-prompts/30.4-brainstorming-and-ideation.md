# Brainstorming and Ideation

28 prompts for unblocking, divergent thinking, and idea exploration.

## Quantity-first ideation

1. Give me 30 ideas for [topic/problem], even bad ones.
2. Generate 20 'how might we [problem]' variations.
3. List 15 different angles to approach [creative brief].
4. Brainstorm 25 metaphors for [abstract concept].
5. Generate 30 candidate titles for [project/piece].
6. List 20 'what if' scenarios about [topic].
7. Brainstorm 15 unexpected pairings combining [A] and [B].

## Forced perspective

8. How would [persona, e.g., a child / a CEO / a poet] solve [problem]?
9. Steal a structure from [unrelated field] to use for [my work].
10. Apply [biological/musical/architectural] principles to [topic].
11. Reframe [problem] as if it were [different domain]'s problem.
12. What would the opposite of [conventional solution] look like?
13. Imagine you're solving [problem] in [different century].
14. How would [famous creator] approach [my project]?

## Combination and constraint

15. Combine 3 random concepts to make a new idea about [topic].
16. What if I had to do [project] with only [constraint]?
17. Reduce [project] to its essence — what's the minimum core?
18. Add a strange constraint to make [problem] more interesting.
19. Generate ideas for [topic] that must include [unexpected element].
20. Mash up [genre A] and [genre B] for a new concept.

## Evaluation and selection

21. Rank these 10 ideas for [criteria like novelty/feasibility]: [paste].
22. Identify the 3 ideas worth prototyping from this list: [paste].
23. Steelman each of these competing ideas: [paste].
24. Find the riskiest assumption behind [idea] worth testing first.
25. Combine the best of [idea A] and [idea B] into [idea C].
26. Suggest which idea to kill — and explain why: [paste list].
27. Frame the 1 question I should be asking before picking an idea.
28. Pre-mortem the idea I'm most excited about: [paste].

**Total prompts: 28**

# 头脑风暴与构想

28 条 prompt: 破除卡点、发散思考、想法探索。

## 先求量

1. 给我 [话题/问题] 的 30 个想法,坏想法也算。
2. 为 [问题] 生成 20 个 'how might we' 变体。
3. 为 [创意命题] 列 15 个不同切入角度。
4. 为 [抽象概念] 头脑风暴 25 个比喻。
5. 为 [项目/作品] 生成 30 个候选标题。
6. 为 [话题] 列 20 个 'what if' 场景。
7. 为 [A] 和 [B] 头脑风暴 15 种意外的组合。

## 强制换视角

8. [小孩/CEO/诗人] 会怎么解决 [问题]?
9. 从 [无关领域] 偷一个结构,用到 [我的工作] 上。
10. 把 [生物学/音乐/建筑] 原理应用到 [话题]。
11. 把 [问题] 重新框定为 [另一领域] 的问题。
12. [常规解决方案] 的反面会是什么样子?
13. 想象你在 [另一个世纪] 解决 [问题]。
14. [著名创作者] 会怎么处理 [我的项目]?

## 组合与约束

15. 为 [话题] 组合 3 个随机概念生成一个新想法。
16. 如果我只有 [约束] 来做 [项目] 呢?
17. 把 [项目] 压到本质 — 最小内核是什么?
18. 为 [问题] 加一个怪约束让它更有趣。
19. 为 [话题] 生成必须含 [意外元素] 的想法。
20. 把 [体裁 A] 和 [体裁 B] 揉在一起生成新概念。

## 评估与挑选

21. 按 [标准如新颖度/可行性] 对这 10 个想法排序: [粘贴]。
22. 在清单中识别值得做原型的 3 个想法: [粘贴]。
23. 为这些竞争想法各打一个有力版本: [粘贴]。
24. 找出 [想法] 背后最该先测试的最大风险假设。
25. 把 [想法 A] 和 [想法 B] 的精华合成 [想法 C]。
26. 建议该砍掉哪个想法,并说明原因: [粘贴清单]。
27. 在挑想法之前我该先问的那 1 个问题是什么?
28. 对我最兴奋的想法做事前 premortem: [粘贴]。
