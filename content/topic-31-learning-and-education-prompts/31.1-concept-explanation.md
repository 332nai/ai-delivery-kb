# Concept Explanation

28 prompts for explaining ideas at different levels of depth and to different audiences.

## Explain at different levels

1. Explain [concept] to a 5-year-old, a teen, an undergrad, and an expert.
2. Give me the ELI5 version of [concept] with a relatable analogy.
3. Explain [advanced topic] using only words a 10-year-old would know.
4. Walk me through [concept] at 3 levels: surface, mechanism, edge cases.
5. Explain [concept] step by step as if I'm taking notes.
6. Give me a 30-second explanation of [topic] for a busy exec.
7. Explain [concept] using a metaphor from [domain].

## Compare and contrast

8. Compare [A] and [B] in a side-by-side table with 5 dimensions.
9. Explain the difference between [commonly confused terms].
10. When should I use [A] vs [B]? Give 3 scenarios for each.
11. Show how [concept] evolved from [predecessor] in 3 stages.
12. List 3 misconceptions about [topic] and the correct view.
13. Map [concept] onto its closest analog in [other field].
14. What's the practical difference between [theory A] and [theory B]?

## Deep dives

15. Teach me [topic] in 30 minutes — what should I read/watch first?
16. List the 10 most important concepts to master [field], in order.
17. Explain the underlying mechanism of [phenomenon] step by step.
18. Walk me through the math behind [formula/algorithm] intuitively.
19. Trace the historical development of [idea] across 5 key thinkers.
20. Identify the open questions / unsolved problems in [field].
21. Explain why [common claim] is more nuanced than it sounds.

## Why does this matter

22. Tell me why [concept] matters in 3 different careers.
23. Give me the 'so what' for [research finding] in plain language.
24. Explain how understanding [topic] would change how I make decisions.
25. List 3 real-world examples where [concept] played a decisive role.
26. Tell me the most counter-intuitive insight in [field].
27. Explain why [problem] is hard, in a way that helps me appreciate it.
28. Suggest 1 question worth asking after I finish learning [topic].

**Total prompts: 28**

# 概念解释

28 条 prompt: 在不同深度、面向不同受众解释概念。

## 用不同深度讲解

1. 用 5 岁、青少年、本科生、专家四种深度解释 [概念]。
2. 用通俗类比给我 [概念] 的 ELI5 版。
3. 只用 10 岁能懂的词解释 [高级话题]。
4. 用 3 层讲 [概念]: 表面、机制、边界情况。
5. 像我在做笔记一样,一步步解释 [概念]。
6. 为忙碌的高管给我 [话题] 的 30 秒解释。
7. 用 [领域] 的比喻解释 [概念]。

## 对比与区分

8. 用 5 个维度做 [A] 和 [B] 的对照表。
9. 解释 [常混淆术语] 之间的区别。
10. [A] 和 [B] 各在什么场景下用? 各举 3 个。
11. 用 3 个阶段展示 [概念] 如何从 [前身] 演化。
12. 列 [话题] 的 3 个误解,以及正确看法。
13. 把 [概念] 映射到 [其他领域] 中最接近的对应物。
14. [理论 A] 和 [理论 B] 在实践上的区别是什么?

## 深入

15. 30 分钟教我 [话题] — 我该先读/看什么?
16. 按顺序列出掌握 [领域] 最重要的 10 个概念。
17. 一步步解释 [现象] 的底层机制。
18. 直觉式地走一遍 [公式/算法] 背后的数学。
19. 通过 5 位关键思想者追溯 [想法] 的历史发展。
20. 识别 [领域] 当前的开放问题/未解问题。
21. 解释为什么 [常见说法] 比听起来更微妙。

## 为什么重要

22. 告诉我 [概念] 在 3 个不同职业中为什么重要。
23. 用通俗语言给我 [研究发现] 的'所以呢'。
24. 解释理解 [话题] 会如何改变我的决策方式。
25. 列 3 个 [概念] 起决定作用的现实案例。
26. 告诉我 [领域] 最反直觉的洞见。
27. 解释 [问题] 为什么难,让我能领会到它的难处。
28. 建议我学完 [话题] 后值得问的 1 个问题。

**共 28 条 prompt**
