# Study Plans and Curricula

28 prompts for designing learning paths from beginner to mastery.

## Path design

1. Design a 30-day study plan for [topic] at 1 hour/day.
2. Build a 6-month curriculum to go from beginner to intermediate in [field].
3. Map a learning path for [skill] including theory, practice, projects.
4. Design a 'just enough' curriculum for [topic] to be productive in 2 weeks.
5. Build a study plan for [exam] using available time of [hours/week].
6. Design a learning sprint structure for [topic] in 2 weeks.
7. Map a curriculum for transitioning from [current field] to [new field].

## Resources

8. Recommend 5 best resources to learn [topic]: book, course, video, blog, tool.
9. Suggest 3 free resources to start [topic] today.
10. Compare 3 popular courses on [topic] by depth, time, and price.
11. Recommend a sequence of books to read on [topic].
12. Find 5 YouTube channels worth subscribing to for [topic].
13. Suggest 5 podcasts that would deepen [my interest].
14. Identify the most influential paper/article to read first in [field].

## Project-based learning

15. Suggest 5 portfolio projects to practice [skill] from beginner to advanced.
16. Design a capstone project that tests mastery of [topic].
17. Generate 3 build-along projects to learn [tool/framework].
18. Suggest a real-world problem I could solve to learn [concept].
19. Design a 'learn in public' plan to ship something every week.
20. Generate 5 weekend project ideas to practice [skill].
21. Build a stretch project that's 30% harder than my current level.

## Assessment

22. Suggest milestones to know I'm progressing in [field].
23. Design a self-assessment for [topic] at beginner/intermediate/advanced.
24. Identify the 'plateau symptoms' in learning [skill] and how to break through.
25. Suggest how to know when I've reached competence in [topic].
26. Build a quarterly learning review template.
27. Identify the next-level skills to learn after I master [current skill].
28. Suggest peer / mentor feedback questions for [skill].

**Total prompts: 28**

# 学习计划与课程

28 条 prompt: 从入门到精通的学习路径设计。

## 路径设计

1. 为 [话题] 设计 30 天、每天 1 小时的学习计划。
2. 构建一份 6 个月课程,把我从 [领域] 入门带到中级。
3. 为 [技能] 规划学习路径: 理论、练习、项目。
4. 为 [话题] 设计一份 2 周能上手的'够用即可'课程。
5. 用每周 [N 小时] 为 [考试] 构建学习计划。
6. 为 [话题] 设计 2 周的学习冲刺结构。
7. 为从 [现领域] 转 [新领域] 规划课程。

## 资源

8. 为学 [话题] 推荐 5 大资源: 书、课、视频、博客、工具。
9. 建议 3 个免费资源,今天就能开始学 [话题]。
10. 按深度、时长、价格对比 3 门 [话题] 热门课程。
11. 推荐一个 [话题] 的书单阅读顺序。
12. 为 [话题] 找 5 个值得订阅的 YouTube 频道。
13. 建议 5 个能加深 [我兴趣] 的播客。
14. 找出 [领域] 最该先读的最具影响力的论文/文章。

## 项目式学习

15. 为练 [技能] 建议 5 个从入门到进阶的作品集项目。
16. 为 [话题] 设计一个能检验掌握度的毕设项目。
17. 为学 [工具/框架] 生成 3 个动手跟做项目。
18. 建议一个我可以解决的现实问题来学 [概念]。
19. 设计一份'公开学习'计划,每周交付一个东西。
20. 为练 [技能] 生成 5 个周末项目点子。
21. 为我设计一个比当前水平难 30% 的拉伸项目。

## 评估

22. 建议判断我在 [领域] 进步的几个里程碑。
23. 为 [话题] 设计入门/中级/高级三档自评。
24. 识别学 [技能] 的'平台期症状'和破局方法。
25. 建议怎么判断我在 [话题] 已达胜任水平。
26. 构建一份季度学习复盘模板。
27. 找出我掌握 [当前技能] 后的下一阶段技能。
28. 为 [技能] 建议向同伴/导师寻求反馈的问题。

**共 28 条 prompt**
