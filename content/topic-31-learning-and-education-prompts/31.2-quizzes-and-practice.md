# Quizzes and Practice

28 prompts for self-testing, problem sets, and active recall.

## Quiz creation

1. Quiz me on [topic] with 10 questions of increasing difficulty.
2. Generate 5 multiple choice questions about [topic] with explanations.
3. Create 5 short answer questions on [topic] for self-testing.
4. Generate questions from [text I just read]: [paste].
5. Create 10 flashcards (Q on front, A on back) for [topic].
6. Quiz me on [topic] using only 'why' and 'how' questions.
7. Generate a closed-book quiz from these notes: [paste].

## Active recall and spaced repetition

8. Quiz me on what I learned yesterday, no hints: [topic].
9. Generate a 'cold quiz' that I haven't seen the source of.
10. Design a spaced repetition schedule for [N] cards over [N] days.
11. Test my retention of [topic] from 1 week ago.
12. Generate retrieval prompts (not multiple choice) for [topic].
13. Create cloze deletions from [paragraph]: [paste].
14. Identify which concepts I likely got wrong based on [my answers].

## Practice problems

15. Generate 5 practice problems for [topic] at [difficulty level].
16. Create an applied scenario problem combining [concept A] and [concept B].
17. Generate a word problem that requires [skill] to solve.
18. Design a problem set with hints that gradually reveal the approach.
19. Generate 5 'common mistake' problems testing [concept].
20. Create a coding exercise to practice [programming concept].
21. Generate 3 case studies that require [framework] to analyze.

## Feedback and explanation

22. Grade my answer to [question]: [paste]. What did I miss?
23. Identify the misconception behind this wrong answer: [paste].
24. Walk through the solution to [problem] step by step.
25. Generate 3 alternative approaches to solving [problem].
26. Explain why my approach to [problem] fails on [edge case].
27. Compare a beginner solution vs an expert solution to [problem].
28. Give me a 'one mistake at a time' practice progression for [skill].

**Total prompts: 28**

# 出题与练习

28 条 prompt: 自测、习题、主动回忆。

## 出题

1. 出 10 题难度递增的 [话题] 测验。
2. 为 [话题] 生成 5 道带解析的选择题。
3. 为 [话题] 创建 5 道自测简答题。
4. 从我刚读的 [文本] 生成问题: [粘贴]。
5. 为 [话题] 制作 10 张抽认卡 (正面问、背面答)。
6. 只用'为什么'和'怎么'类问题考我 [话题]。
7. 用这些笔记生成一份闭卷测验: [粘贴]。

## 主动回忆与间隔重复

8. 考我昨天学的 [话题],不给提示。
9. 出一份我没看过出处的'冷启动测验'。
10. 为 [N] 张卡片在 [N] 天内设计间隔重复计划。
11. 测试我对一周前学的 [话题] 的留存。
12. 为 [话题] 生成检索型题目 (不要选择题)。
13. 从 [段落] 创建填空题: [粘贴]。
14. 基于 [我的答案] 识别我可能答错的概念。

## 练习题

15. 为 [话题] 在 [难度] 生成 5 道练习题。
16. 创建一道把 [概念 A] 和 [概念 B] 结合的应用题。
17. 生成一道需要 [技能] 才能解的应用题。
18. 设计一组带'渐进式提示'的题目。
19. 为 [概念] 生成 5 道'易错题'。
20. 为 [编程概念] 创建一道编程练习。
21. 生成 3 个需要 [框架] 分析的案例研究。

## 反馈与解析

22. 给我对 [问题] 的答案打分: [粘贴]。我漏了什么?
23. 识别这个错误答案背后的误解: [粘贴]。
24. 一步步走一遍 [问题] 的解法。
25. 为 [问题] 生成 3 种替代解法。
26. 解释为什么我对 [问题] 的方法在 [边界情况] 失败。
27. 对比 [问题] 的新手解和专家解。
28. 为 [技能] 给我一份'一次一个错误'的练习推进。

**共 28 条 prompt**
