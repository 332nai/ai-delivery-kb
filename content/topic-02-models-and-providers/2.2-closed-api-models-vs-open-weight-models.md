**Definition**

Closed API models are consumed as a service, you call an endpoint, you pay per token. Open weight models have their parameters published, you download them and run them yourself on your own hardware or cloud. The major open weight families are Llama (Meta), Qwen (Alibaba), DeepSeek (DeepSeek), and Mistral (Mistral AI), with new entrants every few months.

**Why it matters in delivery**

This decision drives architecture, cost, timeline, and team composition. Pick wrong and you spend three months building inference infrastructure you did not need, or you sign a contract that breaks the unit economics at scale. It is one of the highest leverage decisions a PM helps make.

**How it works**

| Factor | Closed API | Open weight self hosted |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first prototype | Hours | 2 to 6 weeks |
| Engineering skills needed | Backend developer | ML engineer, GPU ops, MLOps |
| Cost at low volume (under 1M tokens/day) | Cheap | Expensive (GPU sits idle) |
| Cost at high volume (over 50M tokens/day) | Expensive | Cheap |
| Latency control | Limited, depends on provider | Full control, can optimise |
| Quality at frontier | Highest available | 6 to 12 months behind, gap closing |
| Data leaves environment | Yes (with zero retention options) | No |
| Lock in risk | Medium, prompts may not transfer cleanly | Low, you own the weights |

**PM decision points**

- What is the realistic token volume in 12 to 24 months, not at launch?
- Does the client have ML and infrastructure talent, or will hiring be part of the project?
- Is the quality gap between open and closed acceptable for this specific use case?
- Are there data residency or sovereignty requirements that force self hosting?

**Watch outs**

- Open weight licences vary, Llama has commercial restrictions over a user threshold, Qwen and DeepSeek have their own terms, check before deploying
- "Self hosted" via a cloud provider (Bedrock, Together, Fireworks) is a middle ground, you get open weights without owning the infra
- Performance gaps narrow every quarter, a decision made today should be revisited in 6 months

**Takeaway: closed API is the right default until volume, sovereignty, or customisation forces you to self host. The choice should be driven by numbers, not preference.**

**Qantas story**

Within the Qantas portfolio, there are use cases where the closed vs open weight question genuinely matters and use cases where it does not. The vast majority of customer facing AI work (chat assistants, content generation, knowledge search) lives comfortably on closed API consumption via Bedrock or Azure. The volumes are real but not extreme, and the unit economics work fine on commercial APIs.

The places where open weight earns serious consideration are specific. High volume embedded classification, for example tagging millions of customer service interactions with categories, sentiment, and escalation likelihood, where the workload is predictable and the quality bar can be met with a smaller fine tuned model. Self hosting a fine tuned Llama or Qwen variant for this kind of work can be 5 to 10 times cheaper than API consumption at scale, and the latency profile is more predictable.

Another candidate is anything involving sensitive internal data that the airline's risk function would prefer not to send through any third party processor, even with zero retention agreements in place. Operational safety data, certain crew records, and internal incident reports might fall into this category. The conservative posture is to handle these in a self contained environment, which open weights enable.

What is interesting at Qantas scale is that the open vs closed decision is rarely binary. The pragmatic answer is a portfolio: closed APIs for most workloads, self hosted open weights for the specific cases where economics or sovereignty justify the overhead, and a clear internal framework that helps product teams pick the right path without relitigating the decision every time.

For AI delivery, the lesson is that "should we go open weight" is the wrong question. The right question is "for which specific workload, what does the unit economics and sovereignty profile look like, and does it justify the engineering overhead of self hosting at production grade." That reframing turns a philosophical debate into a per workload analysis that can actually drive a decision. Clients appreciate this approach because it gives them a repeatable framework, not a one off recommendation that becomes stale within six months.

**定义**

Closed API 模型作为服务消费,你调用端点,你按 token 付费。Open weight 模型的参数已发布,你下载并在自己的硬件或云上运行它们。主要的 open weight 家族是 Llama (Meta)、Qwen (阿里巴巴)、DeepSeek (DeepSeek),以及 Mistral (Mistral AI),每隔几个月就有新进入者。

**为何在交付中重要**

这个决定驱动架构、成本、时间表和团队组成。选错,你花三个月构建你不需要的推理基础设施,或者签订一个在规模上破坏单位经济学的合同。这是 PM 帮助做出的最高杠杆决策之一。

**运作原理**

| 因素 | Closed API | Open weight 自托管 |
|---|---|---|
| 第一个原型的时间 | 数小时 | 2 到 6 周 |
| 需要的工程技能 | 后端开发者 | ML 工程师、GPU 运维、MLOps |
| 低量成本 (每天 1M tokens 以下) | 便宜 | 昂贵 (GPU 空闲) |
| 高量成本 (每天 50M+ tokens) | 昂贵 | 便宜 |
| 延迟控制 | 有限,取决于提供商 | 完全控制,可以优化 |
| 前沿质量 | 最高可用 | 落后 6 到 12 个月,差距在缩小 |
| 数据离开环境 | 是 (有零保留选项) | 否 |
| 锁定风险 | 中等,prompts 可能无法干净地转移 | 低,你拥有权重 |

**PM 决策点**

- 12 到 24 个月内的现实 token 量是多少,不是发布时?
- 客户是否有 ML 和基础设施人才,还是招聘将是项目的一部分?
- open 和 closed 之间的质量差距对这个特定用例可接受吗?
- 是否有数据驻留或主权要求强制自托管?

**注意事项**

- Open weight 许可证各异,Llama 在用户阈值以上有商业限制,Qwen 和 DeepSeek 有自己的条款,部署前检查
- 通过云提供商 (Bedrock、Together、Fireworks) "自托管"是中间地带,你获得 open weights 而无需拥有基础设施
- 性能差距每季度缩小,今天做的决定应在 6 个月后重新审视

**要点总结: Closed API 是正确的默认,直到量、主权或定制迫使你自托管。选择应由数字驱动,而非偏好。**

**Qantas 案例**

在 Qantas 组合内,有 closed 与 open weight 问题真正重要的用例,以及不重要的用例。绝大多数面向客户的 AI 工作 (聊天助手、内容生成、知识搜索) 通过 Bedrock 或 Azure 的 closed API 消费很舒适。量是真实的但不极端,单位经济学在商业 API 上运行良好。

open weight 严肃考虑赢得位置的地方是特定的。高量嵌入式分类,例如用类别、情绪和升级可能性标记数百万客户服务互动,工作负载是可预测的,质量门槛可以用更小的 fine tuned 模型满足。在规模上为这种工作自托管 fine tuned Llama 或 Qwen 变体可以比 API 消费便宜 5 到 10 倍,延迟画像更可预测。

另一个候选是涉及敏感内部数据的任何事情,航空公司的风险职能宁愿不通过任何第三方处理器发送,即使有零保留协议。运营安全数据、某些机组记录,以及内部事件报告可能属于此类。保守的姿态是在自包含的环境中处理这些,open weights 使之可能。

在 Qantas 规模上有趣的是,open 与 closed 决策很少是二元的。务实的答案是一个组合: 大多数工作负载使用 closed API,经济学或主权证明开销合理的特定情况下使用自托管 open weights,以及一个清晰的内部框架,帮助产品团队选择正确路径,而无需每次重新审视决策。

对 AI 交付来说,启示是"我们是否应该使用 open weight"是错误的问题。正确的问题是"对哪个特定工作负载,单位经济学和主权画像是什么,以及它是否证明在生产级自托管的工程开销合理"。这种重新框定将哲学辩论转变为可以实际驱动决策的每个工作负载分析。客户欣赏这种方法,因为它给他们一个可重复的框架,而不是六个月内会过时的一次性建议。
