**Definition**

Frontier labs are the small group of organisations training the largest and most capable AI models in the world. The current US set is Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), Google DeepMind (Gemini), xAI (Grok), and Meta (Llama). The China stack is led by DeepSeek, Alibaba (Qwen), Moonshot (Kimi), Zhipu (GLM), and ByteDance (Doubao).

**Why it matters in delivery**

Clients ask "which AI should we use" expecting a one line answer. The honest answer is that each lab has strengths, prices, and risk profiles that suit different use cases. As a PM, you do not need to track every release, but you do need to know who the players are, what they are known for, and which ones are even allowed in your client's environment.

**How it works**

| Lab | Flagship | Known for | Typical enterprise fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | Claude Opus, Sonnet, Haiku | Coding, long context, safety, agentic work | Regulated industries, complex reasoning, agent platforms |
| OpenAI | GPT, o series | Broadest ecosystem, voice, image gen, deepest tooling | General purpose, fastest to prototype, consumer scale |
| Google DeepMind | Gemini | Massive context windows, native multimodal, tight Google Workspace integration | Google Cloud shops, document heavy workloads |
| xAI | Grok | Real time data via X, fewer content guardrails | Niche, social and media use cases |
| Meta | Llama | Open weights, free to self host | Sovereignty, on prem, high volume cost sensitive |
| DeepSeek | V3, R1 | Very strong reasoning at very low cost, open weights | Cost sensitive workloads where China origin is acceptable |
| Alibaba | Qwen | Strong open weight family, excellent Chinese language | China market, multilingual, self hosted |
| Moonshot | Kimi | Long context, agentic capabilities | Chinese enterprise, research |

**PM decision points**

- Which providers does the client already have a contract or cloud relationship with?
- Are there geographic, political, or regulatory constraints (e.g. no China origin models for defence work)?
- Does the use case need a specific strength (coding, long context, multilingual)?

**Watch outs**

- Rankings change every quarter, do not anchor on a benchmark from six months ago
- Many clients have "approved model lists" maintained by their risk team, get this list early
- "We use OpenAI" often means "we use Azure OpenAI," which is a different commercial and data agreement

**Takeaway: there is no best lab, only the best fit for a given client's constraints, contracts, and use case.**

**Qantas story**

When Qantas approaches the question of which frontier lab to partner with, the decision is rarely about benchmarks or model quality. It is about existing cloud agreements, data residency, regulatory posture, and supplier consolidation. The airline has a deep AWS relationship for core systems and a Microsoft 365 estate for productivity. That fact alone narrows the practical model choice for most use cases to Claude via Bedrock, OpenAI via Azure, or open weights via either cloud.

A direct Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google contract would mean a new supplier, new privacy reviews, new payment processes, new account management, and new audit obligations. For an enterprise with sophisticated procurement, that is six months of work that adds little technical benefit. The cloud routes give you the same models with existing commercial paths.

The interesting strategic question for Qantas is not which lab is "best" but which combination provides resilience. Single supplier risk in AI is real. A model version change, a price change, or a service incident from one provider can knock out a production workload. Mature enterprise AI strategies maintain at least two viable providers, with prompt portability tested between them. For Qantas, that probably means Claude as primary and either GPT or Gemini as secondary, both consumed via the existing cloud agreements.

China stack considerations are also live for Qantas in a specific way. The airline serves significant China traffic and has codeshare and operational relationships with Chinese carriers. There is no realistic scenario where Qantas would deploy DeepSeek or Qwen in production for customer facing work, given the political and regulatory complexity. But the technical strength of those models is real, and ignoring them in capability assessments would be naive.

For AI delivery, the broader lesson is that lab choice in large Australian enterprises is decided by procurement and cloud relationships before engineering gets a say. Showing up to a scoping conversation with "we will use the best model for the task" gets you nowhere if the client cannot contract with that lab. The right opening question is "which AI providers can you actually procure today and through which cloud," and the architecture conversation flows from that answer.

**定义**

前沿实验室是世界上能训练最大、最强 AI 模型的一小群组织。目前美国阵营是 Anthropic (Claude)、OpenAI (GPT)、Google DeepMind (Gemini)、xAI (Grok),还有 Meta (Llama)。中国梯队的领头是 DeepSeek、阿里巴巴 (Qwen)、月之暗面 (Kimi)、智谱 (GLM),还有字节跳动 (Doubao)。

**为何在交付中重要**

客户问"我们应该用哪个 AI",期待你给一句话答案。诚实的答案是: 每个实验室都有自己的优势、价格、风险画像,适合不同的用例。作为 PM,你不需要每次发布都追,但你确实需要知道场上有哪几个玩家、各自以什么出名、以及哪些在你客户那边根本不被允许用。

**运作原理**

| 实验室 | 旗舰 | 以什么出名 | 典型企业适用性 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | Claude Opus、Sonnet、Haiku | 编码、长上下文、安全、agentic 工作 | 监管行业、复杂推理、agent 平台 |
| OpenAI | GPT、o 系列 | 最广的生态、语音、图像生成、工具最深 | 通用、最快出原型、消费级规模 |
| Google DeepMind | Gemini | 超大上下文窗口、原生多模态、跟 Google Workspace 深度集成 | Google Cloud 客户、文档密集型工作负载 |
| xAI | Grok | 通过 X 拿实时数据、内容护栏更少 | 小众、社交和媒体用例 |
| Meta | Llama | Open weight、免费自托管 | 数据主权、本地部署、对成本敏感的高量场景 |
| DeepSeek | V3、R1 | 极低成本下能给出非常强的推理、open weight | 能接受中国来源的、成本敏感的工作负载 |
| 阿里巴巴 | Qwen | 强大的 open weight 家族、中文优秀 | 中国市场、多语言、自托管 |
| 月之暗面 | Kimi | 长上下文、agentic 能力 | 中国企业、研究 |

**PM 决策点**

- 客户跟哪些厂商已经有合同或者云关系了?这是第一道筛子,绕不开。
- 有没有地缘政治或监管上的硬约束?比如国防项目,中国来源的模型直接出局,不用讨论。
- 用例是不是需要某个特定的强项 (编码、长上下文、多语言)?如果是,那场上能选的玩家就更窄了。

**注意事项**

- 排名每个季度都在变。六个月前的 benchmark 排名你别拿来当依据。
- 大部分企业都有一份由风险团队维护的"已批准模型清单"。这份清单要尽早拿到手,不然你做完方案才发现首选模型不在表上,白干。
- 客户说"我们用 OpenAI",通常实际意思是"我们用 Azure OpenAI"。这两个在商业合同和数据协议上是完全不同的东西,要分清。

**要点总结: 没有"最佳实验室"这个概念。只有"在这个客户的约束、合同、用例之下,最合适的那一个"。**

**Qantas 案例**

Qantas 在选哪个前沿实验室合作的时候,决策从来不主要是关于 benchmark 或者模型质量。它是关于现有的云合同、数据驻留、监管姿态、供应商整合这些东西。航空公司核心系统用 AWS 很深,生产力工具用 Microsoft 365。光这一件事,就把绝大多数用例的实际模型选择,缩到了三条路: 通过 Bedrock 用 Claude、通过 Azure 用 OpenAI、或者在任一朵云上跑 open weights。

如果想直接跟 Anthropic、OpenAI 或 Google 签合同,意味着: 新供应商、新一轮隐私评审、新支付流程、新客户经理、新一套审计义务。对一家采购流程已经很成熟的企业来说,这是六个月的工作,而且几乎没有技术上的收益——同样的模型,你通过云厂商用,走的是已经打通的商业路径。

对 Qantas 来说,真正有意思的战略问题不是"哪个实验室最好",而是"哪种组合提供弹性"。AI 单一供应商的风险是真实存在的。某个厂商一次模型版本变更、一次涨价、一次服务事故,就可能把你的生产工作负载打掉。一个成熟的企业 AI 战略,至少要同时保有两个可行的厂商,而且要测试 prompt 在两者之间的可移植性。对 Qantas 来说,这大概意味着: Claude 主用、GPT 或 Gemini 备用,两条线都通过现有的云合同消费。

中国梯队这边,对 Qantas 也有特定的考虑方式。航空公司服务大量中国流量,跟中国承运人之间有代码共享和运营关系。但鉴于地缘政治和监管的复杂性,Qantas 不存在任何一个现实场景,会去用 DeepSeek 或 Qwen 做客户侧的工作。话虽如此,这些模型的技术实力是真的,在做能力评估的时候完全忽略它们也是不专业的。

往大了说,这件事给 AI 交付的启示是: 在澳洲大型企业里,实验室的选择,在工程师有发言权之前,就已经被采购和云关系定下来了。你带着"我们用最适合这个任务的模型"这种话去做需求确定,如果客户根本没法跟那个实验室签约,你就一无所获。正确的开场问题是: "你今天实际上能采购到的 AI 厂商有哪些,通过哪朵云?" 架构对话从这个答案才开始。
