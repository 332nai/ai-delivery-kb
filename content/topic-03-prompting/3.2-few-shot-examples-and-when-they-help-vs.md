**Definition**

Few shot prompting is the technique of including a small number of example input output pairs directly in the prompt to show the model what good looks like. Zero shot means no examples, just instructions. One shot is a single example. Few shot is typically 2 to 10 examples.

**Why it matters in delivery**

Examples are one of the highest leverage techniques in prompt engineering. A well chosen example often beats two paragraphs of instructions. But examples cost tokens, anchor the model in specific patterns, and can hurt performance if poorly chosen. Knowing when to add examples and when to remove them is a core PM skill.

**How it works**

| Approach | When to use | Example token cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero shot | Modern frontier models on common tasks | 0 | May miss specific format or edge cases |
| One shot | Showing exact desired format | Low | Model may overfit to that one example |
| Few shot (3 to 5) | Subtle tasks, domain specific tone, edge cases | Medium | Sweet spot for most tasks |
| Many shot (10+) | Classification with many categories, complex extraction | High | Diminishing returns, eats context |

The rules of thumb: examples should be diverse (cover different cases), recent at the end (models weight recent examples more heavily), and labelled clearly (use consistent delimiters like XML tags or markdown).

**Worked example, before and after**

Zero shot prompt:

"Classify this support ticket as billing, technical, or general."

Few shot prompt:

"Classify support tickets. Examples:

Ticket: 'My invoice is wrong' → billing

Ticket: 'App keeps crashing' → technical

Ticket: 'What are your hours' → general

Now classify: [new ticket]"

On a real eval set, this change typically moves accuracy from 70 to 80 percent into the low 90s with no model change.

**PM decision points**

- Has the team tested zero shot before adding examples?
- Are examples diverse and representative, or three near identical cases?
- Where do examples live, and who updates them when the task changes?

**Watch outs**

- Bad examples hurt more than no examples, the model will copy mistakes
- Examples can leak client data into prompts, anonymise carefully
- On very strong models, many shot can underperform a tight zero shot with clear instructions

**Takeaway: start zero shot, add examples to fix specific failures, stop adding when accuracy plateaus.**

**Qantas story**

A useful Qantas example of few shot prompting is in flight delay communication generation. Qantas has a very specific tone for these messages: factual but not robotic, apologetic where appropriate but not grovelling, providing concrete next steps, and never overpromising recovery times. Getting this tone right matters because these messages reach hundreds of thousands of customers across the year, and tone consistency is part of the brand experience.

A zero shot prompt asking for "a delay notification message for a Qantas flight" produces output that is technically correct but often misses the specific Qantas voice. It can read like a generic airline template, sometimes overly formal, sometimes too casual, occasionally apologising too much.

Adding three carefully selected example pairs, real anonymised delay scenarios with the actual messages that Qantas customer communications team produced, transforms the output. The model picks up the rhythm, the specific phrasings, the structure (acknowledge, explain at appropriate detail, provide next steps, close with care), and the things Qantas does not say (no excessive blame on weather, no commitments about specific compensation, no editorialising about the cause).

The interesting subtlety is which examples to choose. Three nearly identical mechanical delay examples teach the model the format but not the range of scenarios. The strong choice is diverse examples: a weather delay, a crew duty time issue, and an aircraft swap. Each represents a different category, and the model learns to handle the family of situations, not just one type.

The maintenance cost is real. When the Qantas customer communications team evolves its style guide (which happens every few quarters), the examples need to be updated. The eval set needs to be rerun. The change management discipline is the same as any production prompt.

For AI delivery, the broader pattern is that few shot examples are the most efficient way to encode tacit knowledge that lives in a specific team. A communications team has style preferences that no system prompt fully captures in instructions, but three good examples make implicit knowledge explicit to the model. The skill is in example selection and curation, which is more of a content design discipline than a technical one. The teams that nail this combine product, content, and engineering in ways that traditional software delivery does not.

**定义**

Few shot prompting 是一种技术: 在 prompt 里直接塞几个"输入-输出"配对的示例,给模型展示"什么样的输出是好的"。零样本 (zero shot) 意思是不给示例,只给指令。一次示例 (one shot) 是给 1 个示例。Few shot 通常是 2 到 10 个示例。

**为何在交付中重要**

示例是 prompt engineering 里杠杆最高的技术之一。一个精挑过的示例通常胜过两段指令。但示例要烧 tokens,会把模型锚定到特定 pattern,选不好还会伤害表现。知道什么时候加示例、什么时候要砍掉示例,是 PM 的核心技能。

**运作原理**

| 方法 | 什么时候用 | Token 成本 | 风险 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 零样本 | 现代前沿模型 + 常见任务 | 0 | 可能漏特定格式或边缘情况 |
| 一次示例 | 想精确展示期望的格式 | 低 | 模型可能过拟合到那一个示例 |
| Few shot (3 到 5 个) | 微妙的任务、领域特定的语气、边缘情况 | 中 | 大多数任务的最优点 |
| 多次示例 (10+ 个) | 多类别分类、复杂提取 | 高 | 边际收益递减,而且吃上下文 |

经验法则: 示例要多样化 (覆盖不同情况)、把最重要的放最后 (模型更重视最近看到的示例)、并清晰标记 (用一致的分隔符,比如 XML 标签或 markdown)。

**举例说明,前后对比**

零样本 prompt:

"把这张支持工单分类为账单、技术、或一般。"

Few shot prompt:

"对支持工单做分类。示例:

工单: '我的发票错了' → 账单

工单: '应用一直崩溃' → 技术

工单: '你们的营业时间是什么' → 一般

现在分类: [新工单]"

在真实的 eval 集上,光做这一个改动,准确率经常从 70-80% 跳到 90 多,而且模型本身没换。

**PM 决策点**

- 团队加示例之前,有没有先把零样本测一遍?跳过这一步就直接加示例,你不知道是不是在解决一个不存在的问题。
- 示例是多样化、有代表性的,还是三个几乎一样的案例?
- 示例存在哪里?任务变化的时候谁负责更新?这件事不定下来,几个月之后示例就跟现实脱节了。

**注意事项**

- 坏示例比不给示例更糟糕。模型会原样把错误学过去。
- 示例可能会把客户数据泄漏进 prompt,要仔细做匿名化处理。
- 在非常强的模型上,堆很多示例的效果反而不如一个紧凑的、指令清晰的零样本。

**要点总结: 从零样本开始,只为修复具体的失败再加示例,准确率平稳之后就别再加了。**

**Qantas 案例**

few shot prompting 在 Qantas 一个很实用的例子是航班延误通知生成。Qantas 对这类消息有非常具体的语气要求: 事实但不机械、该道歉的时候道歉但不卑躬屈膝、提供具体的下一步、永远不要在恢复时间上过度承诺。这种语气做对很重要,因为这些消息一年要送到几十万客户面前,语气一致性本身就是品牌体验的一部分。

零样本的 prompt "写一条 Qantas 航班延误通知",产出的东西技术上正确,但通常抓不到那种特有的 Qantas 调调。读起来像一份通用航司模板: 有时候太正式,有时候太随意,偶尔道歉过度。

加进去三个精心挑过的示例对——真实的、脱敏过的延误场景,配上 Qantas 客户沟通团队实际产出的消息——输出就变了。模型抓到了节奏、特定的措辞、那个结构 (先承认、按合适程度做解释、提供下一步、用关怀收尾),也抓到了 Qantas 不会说的东西 (不会把锅完全甩给天气、不会承诺具体的补偿、不会对原因做评论)。

有意思的微妙之处在于"选哪三个示例"。三个几乎一样的机械故障延误,只能教会模型格式,教不会场景范围。强的选法是多样性: 一个天气延误、一个机组值勤时间问题、一次飞机调换。每一个代表一类不同的情况,模型学到的是"如何处理一族场景",而不是"只会处理一种"。

维护成本是真的。Qantas 客户沟通团队每隔几个季度会更新一次风格指南,那个时候示例要跟着改,eval 集要跟着重跑。变更管理的纪律,跟其他任何生产级 prompt 是一样的。

往大了说,这件事给 AI 交付的启示是: few shot 示例是把"一个团队头脑里的隐性知识"编码到模型里最有效的方式。沟通团队对风格有一堆偏好,这些偏好用 system prompt 的指令是写不全的,但三个好的示例就能把这种隐性知识对模型变成显性的。技巧在于"挑示例和策划示例"这件事,这件事更多是内容设计的功夫,不是技术功夫。这一块做得好的团队,会以传统软件交付里见不到的方式,把产品、内容、工程绑在一起。
