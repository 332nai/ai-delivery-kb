# Decisions and Thinking

26 prompts for decision frameworks, mental models, and reflection.

## Decision frameworks

1. Apply 10/10/10 to this decision: [decision]. How will I feel in 10 mins/months/years?
2. Identify if this is a Type 1 (reversible) or Type 2 (irreversible) decision: [paste].
3. Build a decision matrix for [options] across [criteria].
4. Run a pre-mortem on this decision: [decision]. What would make it fail?
5. Apply Charlie Munger's inversion: what would guarantee the worst outcome?
6. List 5 second-order consequences of [decision] I might miss.
7. Generate a 'kill criteria' I'd accept for [project/decision].
8. Apply Bayesian reasoning to [hypothesis] given [evidence].

## Mental models

9. Frame [problem] using first principles — strip away assumptions.
10. Apply opportunity cost reasoning to [choice].
11. Use Occam's razor on [competing explanations] for [observation].
12. Identify which Mental model applies best to [situation]: [shortlist].
13. Apply the 80/20 rule to [my activities] last month.
14. Frame [trade-off] as a hedge vs concentration decision.
15. Apply game theory: how do the other parties' incentives shape [decision]?

## Reflection and journaling

16. Generate 5 reflection questions to journal on at end of [day/week].
17. Help me extract 3 lessons from [recent event/project].
18. Conduct a personal retrospective on [time period]: what worked, what didn't.
19. Identify patterns in my recent decisions — biases I keep making.
20. Write a 'letter to my future self' about [decision/goal].
21. Conduct a year-in-review covering wins, lessons, regrets, intentions.
22. Suggest journaling prompts for working through [emotion/challenge].

## Saying yes and no

23. Help me decide whether to accept [opportunity] using these criteria: [list].
24. Draft a polite no to [request] preserving the relationship.
25. Identify what I should stop doing to make space for [priority].
26. Run a 'hell yes or no' filter on these 5 invites: [list].

**Total prompts: 26**

# 决策与思考

26 条 prompt: 决策框架、心智模型、反思。

## 决策框架

1. 用 10/10/10 分析这个决策: [决策]。10 分钟/月/年后我会怎么想?
2. 判断这是可逆 (一类) 还是不可逆 (二类) 决策: [粘贴]。
3. 为 [选项] 按 [标准] 构建决策矩阵。
4. 对这个决策做事前 premortem: [决策]。哪些会让它失败?
5. 用芒格的'逆向思维': 怎样能确保最坏结果?
6. 为 [决策] 列 5 个我可能漏掉的二阶后果。
7. 为 [项目/决策] 生成我能接受的'止损线'。
8. 对 [假设] 基于 [证据] 应用贝叶斯推理。

## 心智模型

9. 用第一性原理重新表述 [问题] — 剥离假设。
10. 对 [选择] 应用机会成本推理。
11. 用奥卡姆剃刀处理 [观察] 的 [竞争性解释]。
12. 判断哪个心智模型最适合 [情境]: [候选清单]。
13. 对我上月 [活动] 应用 80/20 法则。
14. 把 [权衡] 表述成对冲 vs 集中的决策。
15. 应用博弈论: 其他参与者的激励如何影响 [决策]?

## 反思与日记

16. 为 [日/周] 末的反思日记生成 5 个问题。
17. 帮我从 [近期事件/项目] 提取 3 条教训。
18. 为 [时间段] 做个人复盘: 什么有效、什么没效。
19. 识别我近期决策的模式 — 我反复犯的偏见。
20. 为 [决策/目标] 写一封'给未来自己的信'。
21. 做一份年度回顾: 胜利、教训、遗憾、意图。
22. 为消化 [情绪/挑战] 建议日记问题。

## 说是与说不

23. 用这些标准帮我决定要不要接受 [机会]: [列表]。
24. 起草一份礼貌拒绝 [请求] 的措辞,保住关系。
25. 找出该停止做什么以腾出空间给 [优先事项]。
26. 用'要么超想要么不要'筛子过滤这 5 个邀请: [列表]。
