# Time and Schedule

26 prompts for planning days, weeks, deep work, and calendar audits.

## Daily and weekly planning

1. Plan my day given these [N] tasks and [N] meetings: [paste].
2. Build a weekly plan around [3 priorities] and existing commitments: [paste].
3. Identify the one most important thing I should do tomorrow given [goals].
4. Block-schedule my [day/week] separating deep work from shallow work.
5. Propose a morning routine for [type of work] in 60 minutes.
6. Suggest the 3 most important tasks for this week given [context]: [paste].
7. Draft a Sunday weekly preview based on [next week's commitments].
8. Build an end-of-day shutdown routine to leave work behind.

## Deep work and focus

9. Design a 90-minute deep work block for [task] with prep and recovery.
10. List the 5 distractions I should remove before starting [project].
11. Suggest a Pomodoro variant suited to [my work type and energy].
12. Identify when in my day I'm probably at peak energy for [task type].
13. Build a focus playlist / environment checklist for [task].
14. Suggest a single-tasking constraint that would help me ship [project].
15. Design a 'no meetings before noon' negotiation with my team.

## Calendar audit

16. Audit my last week's calendar — flag low-value time: [paste].
17. Suggest 3 recurring meetings to kill, keep, or change frequency.
18. Identify pockets of fragmented time on my calendar I can consolidate.
19. Compare time spent on [strategic vs operational] work last month: [paste].
20. Suggest how to defragment my calendar to get 2 deep work blocks per day.
21. Build a 'meeting-free Wednesday' proposal for my team.
22. Identify which meetings I attend that could be an email instead.

## Energy and recovery

23. Match these [tasks] to high/medium/low energy slots in my day.
24. Suggest mini-recovery rituals between back-to-back meetings.
25. Build a weekly recovery plan including sleep, exercise, social.
26. Identify the 3 biggest energy drains in my current week: [paste].

# 时间与日程

26 条 prompt: 规划日、周、深度工作时间、日程审计。

## 日 / 周规划

1. 基于这 [N] 个任务和 [N] 个会议为我规划今天: [粘贴]。
2. 围绕 [3 个优先事项] 和现有承诺规划本周: [粘贴]。
3. 基于 [目标] 找出明天最重要的一件事。
4. 给我的 [天/周] 排时间块,把深度工作和浅工作分开。
5. 为 [工作类型] 提议一份 60 分钟的晨间例程。
6. 基于 [背景] 为本周建议 3 个最重要任务: [粘贴]。
7. 基于 [下周承诺] 起草一份周日的周预览。
8. 设计一份能下班放下工作的结束例程。

## 深度工作与专注

9. 为 [任务] 设计一个 90 分钟深度工作块,含准备和恢复。
10. 在开始 [项目] 前列出 5 个该消除的干扰。
11. 为 [我的工作类型和精力] 建议合适的番茄工作法变体。
12. 判断一天中我大概什么时间精力最佳,适合 [任务类型]。
13. 为 [任务] 建一份专注的播放列表/环境清单。
14. 建议一个能帮我交付 [项目] 的单任务限制规则。
15. 设计一份'上午不开会'的团队协商方案。

## 日程审计

16. 审计我上周日程,标出低价值时间: [粘贴]。
17. 建议 3 个周期会议: 砍掉 / 保留 / 改频率。
18. 在我日程里识别可整合的碎片时间。
19. 对比我上月花在 [战略 vs 运营] 工作的时间: [粘贴]。
20. 建议如何整理日程,每天留出 2 个深度工作块。
21. 为团队起草'周三无会日'的提议。
22. 识别哪些我参加的会议其实可以改成邮件。

## 精力与恢复

23. 把这些 [任务] 匹配到一天中精力高/中/低的时段。
24. 为连背会议之间建议小型恢复仪式。
25. 构建一份周度恢复计划,含睡眠、运动、社交。
26. 找出我本周 3 个最大的精力黑洞: [粘贴]。
