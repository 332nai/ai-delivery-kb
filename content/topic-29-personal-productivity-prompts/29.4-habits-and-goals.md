# Habits and Goals

26 prompts for setting goals, building habits, and sustaining change.

## Goal setting

1. Convert this vague goal [goal] into a SMART version.
2. Break this annual goal into quarterly outcomes: [goal].
3. Identify 3 leading indicators for [goal] besides the outcome metric.
4. Write OKRs for [role/project] this quarter: 1 objective, 3 key results.
5. Find the smallest version of [goal] I could ship in a week.
6. Identify the 1 keystone goal that would make others easier.
7. Stress test [goal] — what has to be true for it to be reached?

## Habit design

8. Design a tiny habit for [desired behavior] using BJ Fogg's method.
9. Identify the cue/routine/reward loop for [habit I'm building].
10. Find an existing habit to anchor [new habit] to (habit stacking).
11. Design an environment hack that makes [habit] inevitable.
12. Plan a 'minimum viable streak' for [habit] over [N] days.
13. Identify the friction in current attempt at [habit].
14. Build a habit tracker for [habit] with a recovery rule for misses.

## Breaking habits

15. Design a replacement for [bad habit] that meets the same need.
16. Identify the trigger that precedes [unwanted behavior].
17. Build a friction wall around [habit I want to reduce].
18. Suggest a 30-day experiment to test if quitting [habit] is worth it.
19. Plan accountability for breaking [habit] using a [partner/app].

## Sustaining and reviewing

20. Build a weekly review of [habit/goal] progress in 10 minutes.
21. Plan how to restart [habit] after a long break without shame.
22. Identify signals that [goal] is no longer worth pursuing.
23. Build a quarterly 'goal hygiene' review template.
24. Suggest celebration rituals for milestones in [goal].
25. Design a 'second-half push' protocol for goals at midpoint.
26. Generate a list of identity statements for [aspiration].

**Total prompts: 26**

# 习惯与目标

26 条 prompt: 定目标、建习惯、维持改变。

## 目标设定

1. 把这个模糊目标 [目标] 转成 SMART 版本。
2. 把这个年度目标拆成季度结果: [目标]。
3. 为 [目标] 在结果指标之外识别 3 个先行指标。
4. 为本季度的 [角色/项目] 写 OKR: 1 个目标、3 个关键结果。
5. 找出 [目标] 在一周内能交付的最小版本。
6. 找出一个能让其他目标更容易达成的'拱顶目标'。
7. 压力测试 [目标] — 要达成必须哪些为真?

## 习惯设计

8. 用 BJ Fogg 方法为 [目标行为] 设计微习惯。
9. 为 [我在建的习惯] 找出 提示-行为-奖励 回路。
10. 为 [新习惯] 找一个可锚定的已有习惯 (习惯堆叠)。
11. 设计一个让 [习惯] 不可避免的环境技巧。
12. 为 [习惯] 在 [N] 天内规划一个'最小可行连续'。
13. 找出我当前尝试 [习惯] 时的阻力。
14. 为 [习惯] 建一个带'漏一次怎么恢复'规则的追踪器。

## 戒除习惯

15. 为 [坏习惯] 设计一个满足同样需求的替代。
16. 识别 [不想要的行为] 之前的触发点。
17. 为 [想减少的习惯] 建一道阻力墙。
18. 设计一个 30 天实验,测试戒掉 [习惯] 是否值得。
19. 用 [伙伴/App] 为戒除 [习惯] 规划问责。

## 维持与复盘

20. 为 [习惯/目标] 进展构建 10 分钟内的周复盘。
21. 规划如何在长时间中断后无负担地重启 [习惯]。
22. 识别 [目标] 不再值得追求的信号。
23. 构建一份季度'目标卫生'复盘模板。
24. 为 [目标] 的里程碑建议庆祝仪式。
25. 为半程的目标设计一份'下半场冲刺'方案。
26. 为 [渴望] 生成一组身份陈述句。

**共 26 条 prompt**
